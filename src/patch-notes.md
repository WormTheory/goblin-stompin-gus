# Patch notes

## Version 0.3

### Highlights

- Play online: up to four players in one city over the internet. One hosts, and the others pick the game from the Open games list, or type in its room code for a private game. Find it under Play online on the main menu.
- Text chat and opt-in voice chat in online games.
- Two players can share one armed vehicle, one driving and one on the gun.
- Police draw handguns at range, and every goblin attack has a proper wind-up and strike.

### A new look

- The game has a new coat of paint: flat crayon colours, thick ink outlines with hand-drawn corners, and a chunky pixel font (Jersey 10) for titles, buttons and numbers. Anything you read, like descriptions and these notes, keeps the easy-to-read font.
- Button prompts are drawn as the buttons themselves in the new style: crayon-coloured face buttons and paper keycaps.
- A real title screen: the logo top left, and Gus himself stomping along a street in front of a scrolling city while you choose. The menu card on the right has Play, Co-op, Play online, New city and Options, with the Testing Environment, Dev and Full screen in a small Workshop line underneath, and a sticker for what's new.
- The enemy hostility slider has moved from the main screen to the top of Options.
- Picking a character is now "Who's stompin'?": four big cards for Gus, Gertie, Gnash and Grizzle, with a 1P tag on player one's pick and a 2P tag on player two's. Choose which player you're picking for at the top (player two's own menu always picks for player two), and a strip under the cards lists what that character can do with your current ability switches. Picking the other player's character still swaps you over.
- On a controller, A on a character card picks them and takes you back ("Ready!"), and Y jumps down to the ability switches. Screens opened from a menu have a Back button: B or Esc goes back, and LB / RB (Q / E on the keyboard) jump between screens.
- The pause menu is a column of big buttons (Resume, Map, Obituary with its count, Character, Controls, Options, Quit to title) beside a "This rampage" card: goblins flattened, your score, your best combo, cars tossed and how wrecked the place you're in is, with its name and the time of day. Underneath are this game's own controls: switch between solo and co-op, Play online, New city, the Testing Environment and Full screen.
- Map, Obituary, Character, Controls, Options and Patch notes open in the same card, sliding in from the right, and always start you back on the menu's own buttons when you pause again. B or Esc on the menu itself resumes.
- Quit to title takes you back to the title screen with Gus stomping along; your city, score and everyone in it are kept, so Play carries on where you left off.
- Patch notes and Dev are small tags in the card's corner now. The card's corner says 1P MENU (green) or 2P MENU (pink), and player two's menu still leaves out Options, Dev, the Testing Environment, Quit to title and the city controls.
- Options, Controls, the Map, the Obituary and the Patch notes are on paper in the new style. The Dev tools and the Testing Environment keep a dark panel, in the new colours.

### Split-screen co-op menus

- Both players get the full pause menu now. Press Start to pause your own half of the screen, then Y (or click Pause menu) to open the menu as yours. Its corner says whose menu it is, and only that player's controller moves around it.
- Player two's menu leaves out what changes the game for both of you: Game options, the Dev tab, the Testing Environment, and the new city, solo, online and hostility controls. Those stay with player one, and so does the debug menu on Select.
- In player two's menu, the button that carries on playing says Resume rather than Co-op.
- Picking characters has moved to the Character tab, and the Game tab just says who you're playing as, with a link there.
- Each player can now be any of the four: Gus, Gertie, Gnash or Grizzle. Pick the one the other player has and you swap over, so you can always tell each other apart. Grizzle wears red, Gnash blue.

### Online co-op

- The host's city is the one you play in. Joining builds it in your browser without touching your own city, and leaving takes you back to yours.
- Everything the host's game does shows up for the guest: goblins, traffic, trains, planes, boats, rides, smashed blocks, toppling buildings, fires, dust and sound.
- Your own hippo moves the moment you press, even on a slow connection, and is nudged back into step with the host's game if the two drift apart.
- Bites and stomps land on goblins where you saw them, not where they've moved to since.
- Play Gnash online and you can drive, with the chase camera following you.
- The host can switch to another tab or window and the game carries on for everyone else, so you can also try it out in two tabs of one browser (tick "Two tabs of this browser" in both).
- Drop out and you're put straight back in your place when the connection comes back (within half a minute).
- Each player keeps the abilities switched on in their own browser. The host's hostility setting is the game's, and the world never pauses while you're both in it.
- If you both pick the same hippo, the guest plays the other one, so you can tell each other apart.

### Chat and voice chat online

- Talk to the others in an online game: press Enter, type a message and press Enter again to send it (Esc cancels). Messages show at the bottom left with the sender's name in their colour and fade away after a few seconds. Press Enter again to read back through the recent ones.
- While you're typing, your keys stay in the chat box: Gus or Gertie stands still instead of walking off.
- On a controller, D-pad ↑ brings up a few quick messages ("Over here!", "Help!", "Follow me!" and more): pick one with the D-pad and press A to send it, or B to put the list away.
- Messages are up to 120 characters, and only ever shown as plain text. Typing very fast gets a "slow down" instead.
- Voice chat, for games over the internet: switch it on in Options, under Online chat & voice. It's off until you switch it on, and it's off again every time you start the game. Nothing touches your microphone until then, and your browser asks first.
- Push to talk is the default: hold T (D-pad ← on a controller) to talk. Untick it for an open microphone. Both buttons can be changed in the Controls tab.
- Little chips at the bottom left show who's on voice and light up while they're talking. You can mute any player just for yourself, and set how loud voices are.
- You only hear players who have voice chat switched on, and with yours off nobody sends you any sound at all.
- No microphone? You can still listen. Tick "Listen only" and voice chat never asks for a microphone: you hear everyone who's talking, with their chips lighting up, mute and volume as usual. If your microphone is missing or you don't allow it, voice chat switches to listening only by itself and says "No microphone: listening only".
- Don't want chat? Untick "Show chat" in Options to hide it altogether, or "Other players' messages" to mute what the others type.
- Enter can't be picked for other controls any more, as it opens the chat.

### Open games

- No more reading out room codes: Play online now lists the games being hosted right now under Open games, with who's hosting and how many players are in. Click Join on one to jump in.
- Host a game and it shows up on everyone else's list straight away. It drops off when you stop hosting, and shows as Full once all four seats are taken.
- Want to play with just your friends? Tick "Private game" before or while hosting and your game stays off the list. They can still join with the room code, which works just as before.
- Works with "Two tabs of this browser" too, for trying it out on one computer.

### Goblin attacks

- Every goblin attack now has a proper wind-up and a strike that suits the weapon. Nothing hits any sooner or later than before: you just get a better look at it coming.
- Goblins and police lift their club up behind their heads, pause at the top, then chop it down, turning into the blow.
- Thieves and cultists draw their dagger back to the hip, point first, then thrust it straight in at arm's length.
- Preppers, militiamen and soldiers swing their rifle round so the stock faces forward, then shove it into you with both hands.
- Rifle shots: the rifle is brought up to the shoulder, held on you, then kicks when it fires.
- Grenades are pulled back behind the head and thrown overarm, and cultists raise their fireball high as it grows, then hurl it.
- Gnash and Grizzle swing the same way: a club drawn back and chopped down, a dagger drawn back and thrust, in your own view and as other players see you. Their arm follows through after a grenade throw.
- The Dev tab's Enemies viewer marks each attack's wind-up, strike and follow-through on the timeline.

### Police handguns

- Police now carry a handgun in a holster on their belt. Once they've spotted you, they draw it and shoot from 4 to 30 blocks away, then holster it again. Get in close and they swap back to the club.
- They hold it out in both hands and take aim for a moment first, so you can see the shot coming. When it fires, it kicks their wrists up.
- Police who come out fighting after you carjack their car carry one too, and so do the Testing Environment's police officers.
- With enemy hostility turned down, police keep their guns holstered.
- The Dev tab's Enemies viewer has a new "Draw and fire the handgun" animation for police officers.
- Online, guests see the gun drawn and the holster, and now see every bullet's tracer, the rifles' too.

### Driver and gunner

- Two goblins can now share one armed vehicle. While one player drives, the other walks up and presses the get-in button to take the gunner's seat. Both local and online co-op work.
- Technicals and four-wheelers: the gunner works the machine gun or the rifle, aiming wherever they look while the driver steers. You see them standing at the gun in the truck bed, or sitting behind the rider.
- Tanks and helicopters: the gunner aims the turret, or the helicopter's gun and rockets, with their own view while the driver steers or flies.
- On your own, the weapon button switches between the driver's seat and the gun, so you can man a parked technical's gun yourself. If the driver gets out, the gunner can keep shooting from where the vehicle stopped.
- The gunner's machine gun has a 50-round belt, and the four-wheeler's rifle holds 6 rounds. Both reload by themselves.
- A goblin under your crosshair is now what vehicle guns aim at, rather than the ground behind it, so shots from a gun mounted off to one side don't miss.

### Goblins climbing after you

- Goblins climbing after you follow you round the corner of a building on the wall. They no longer climb all the way to the roof first and then come back down.
- If there's no way round on the wall, a goblin below you climbs back down and runs round to your side when that's quicker than going over the top.
- A goblin on the roof above you climbs down over the edge to get you. It no longer runs off the edge or gets stuck up there.

### Menus

- Press Esc in the pause menu to carry on playing, the same as pressing Play. It captures the mouse again where the browser allows it; if it doesn't, the usual hint asks you to click once. In co-op, Esc resumes just your own hippo. On the "start a new city?" question, Esc answers No first.

### Bug fixes

- Fixed flickering on the striped umbrellas over café tables, on beaches and in parks.
- A collapsing building now falls toward the side where its outer walls were smashed. Staircases, pillars, inside walls and furniture no longer pull it one way or another, and a building whose outer walls are still standing all round comes straight down.

## Version 0.2

Everything since the first release on GitHub.

### Highlights

- A character select on the main menu: play as Gus, Gertie, or Gnash the goblin.
- Every vehicle now collides with every other, and a driven tank smashes straight through walls.
- Enemy hostility is a three-step slider: Passive, Peaceful or Normal.
- Goblins climb walls after Gus, and long falls now hurt them.
- These patch notes are now in the main menu and on their own page on the website.

### Characters

- **Character select:** pick Gus, Gertie or Gnash on the title screen, and the game remembers your choice. Arrow keys and the d-pad move along the cards.
- **Gnash** is the playable goblin, and replaces the old "Play as a goblin" debug switch. In co-op both players are goblins: Gnash and his cousin Grizzle.
- **Gertie** can now be played solo. In co-op, whoever player one picks, player two is the other hippo.
- Gus and Gertie are the same hippo in everything but looks and voice: every move works exactly the same for both.
- Climbing as Gnash no longer lets you see through the wall into the building. The view stays outside on walls, round corners and under ceilings.
- **Gnash can dash:** tap Shift (pad B) to dash. With no direction held he dashes the way he's looking.
- **Gnash's weapons:** besides his club he can carry a dagger (quick stabs) and a rifle (shoots where you aim, with a crosshair). Kill an enemy carrying a weapon you haven't got and they drop it; walk over it to pick it up. R (pad d-pad right) switches weapons, and the bar by your hearts shows what you're carrying. In co-op, the other player sees the weapon in Gnash's hand.
- **Grenades** stack, up to 9. Soldiers, preppers and militia drop them, and Gnash throws one with stomp (right click, RT). Pipe bombs are gone: preppers throw grenades too.
- The controls hint shows Gnash's own moves while you play as him.
- **Mixed co-op:** player two can now be a hippo or a goblin, whoever player one picked. Choose it in the "Co-op, player two" row under the character cards: Gus and Gnash can play together, each with their own moves, camera and controls hint.

### Goblins and enemies

- Goblins chasing Gus up a wall climb after him, and club him once they're level instead of waiting below.
- Falls of more than 6 blocks hurt goblins, a point for every 2 blocks more, so a 16-block drop kills one. Water breaks the fall, and their routes no longer jump down drops that would hurt.
- **Enemy hostility** is now a slider on the main screen and in the quick debug menu. Passive: nobody reacts to you, even when hit. Peaceful: nobody attacks, but people still run. Normal: they come for you. An old Peaceful setting carries over.
- Playing as a goblin no longer calms everyone down. Hostility works just as it does for Gus.
- **Crews:** a base's tanks and helicopters and a militia camp's vehicles now start empty. When the camp is set off, soldiers run out of the barracks and militiamen out of the HQ, climb aboard, and only then do the vehicles start up. Shoot one on the way and its vehicle waits for the next. When a crewed vehicle is destroyed, its crew's bodies are thrown out.

### Vehicles

- **Carjacking:** playing as Gnash, getting into a vehicle that has someone at the controls hauls them out first. A car's door swings open, a motorcycle's rider is shoved off, a tank's crewman is dragged out of the hatch and a helicopter's door slides back. Whoever's thrown out, a driver, a police officer or a soldier, comes back at you or runs for it.
- All vehicles now collide with each other. Tanks no longer drive through a helicopter's tail, trains, planes, boats and rides push tanks and cars out of the way, and driven planes and boats stop or crash when they meet another vehicle.
- A driven tank ramming a wall knocks out a hole as big as its front and keeps going at speed. It also fits under overhangs it can pass beneath.
- Firing with nothing loaded just clicks, and the tank's last shell marker empties while it reloads.
- The vehicle health box and ammo panel now sit just above the hearts.
- The get-out prompt shows Xbox button labels on Xbox controllers.
- Parked motorcycles no longer have a rider, and a ridden one's rider sits properly on it.
- **Planes fly properly:** a driven plane has throttle, banks to turn, pitches up and down, gets its lift from airspeed, and stalls if it flies too slowly.
- **Fire engines** have a working water cannon.
- **Militia camps** have two technicals (pickups with a machine gun and a gunner in the back) and two four-wheelers (a rider and a rifleman behind him). Each carries a driver and a gunner. Once crewed they circle you while the gunner fires. Gnash can carjack them, and the gunner jumps off to fight. The Testing Environment has buttons for both.

### Movement

- Air dash comes back after Gus leaves the water, and can't be used while swimming.
- Holding jump keeps you swimming up.
- **Fighting from a wall:** while spider-climbing a wall or the side of a vehicle, R1 (left click) snaps at goblins the way the hippo's head points: up, along or down. It bites vehicles in reach too, including the one you're clinging to. Gnash swings or shoots whatever he's holding.
- **Kick through a wall:** stomp while spider-climbing smashes a hole in the wall you're on and puts you inside, on the floor behind it. Into solid rock, it kicks out a hollow and you keep climbing, so you can tunnel in.

### World

- The pavement stairwells down to the sewers are gone. The sewers are still under every town street, but the only way in now is to smash through.
- Subway station stairs are built from real stair blocks, so the way down to the platforms is a smooth flight of steps instead of a staircase of plain blocks.

### Menus and debug tools

- A Patch notes tab in the main menu, with a "What's new" line on the title screen, and the same notes on the website at /patch-notes/.
- Earlier versions can be played on the website: each one has a Play button beside its heading in the patch notes. An old version keeps its own saves and settings, apart from the current game's, and a badge at the top links back to the latest.
- A Sound list overlay names every sound as it plays, each by its own name: a tank firing, a dry-fire click, a reload and a helicopter's gun no longer show up as "Stone breaks" after something stony has been smashed.
- In the quick debug menu, pick a slider with A before left and right move it, like the other menus.
- The Testing Environment's spawning menu is its own tab, just right of Game, so the Game tab stays the normal title screen.
- The text above the Enemy hostility slider describes only the step you've picked.
- The quick debug menu has a second tab, Show / hide (LB / RB or Tab), for choosing which switches the first tab lists, with Show all and Hide all at the top. Your choice is remembered.
- The debug switches are only in the quick debug menu now. Game options keeps just the switch that allows it, which also shows or hides the Dev tab.
- Button names on the screen (the controls hint, vehicle prompts, the weapon panel) are drawn as the same button pictures the menus use, for the keyboard, mouse or controller you're on.
- The Dev tab's move preview has a character picker in its top left corner: Gus, Gertie or Gnash. Gnash shows his own moves (walk, dash, run, jump and club swing) on the goblin model, with their timings beside him.
- A new Enemies section in the Dev tab plays each enemy's animations frame by frame: goblins, police officers, civilians, cultists, thieves, preppers and militiamen. Pick the enemy and the animation from the menus in its top left corner. It shows their hits, shots, grenades and fireballs, a timeline marking wind-ups and hits, and a frame data panel with damage, timings and ranges.

### Bug fixes

- In co-op, Gnash's body now turns to face where he's looking, and falls flat when he dies. Before, it always faced the same way.
- Blood splats that overlap no longer flicker.
- Pausing now freezes the whole city: a falling hippo or goblin hangs in mid-air, and cars, fires, rubble and everyone else stop until you resume. Pausing both players in co-op freezes it too.
- The mouse no longer gets stuck moving a cursor during play. If the game loses the mouse (for example by resuming within a second of pressing Esc), a hint asks you to click, and one click takes it back.
- In full screen on Chrome and Edge, tapping Esc now pauses the game without leaving full screen. Hold Esc to exit full screen. Other browsers still leave full screen on Esc, as they don't let games keep that key.

## Version 0.1

Everything from the move to Claude Code on November 2, 2026, up to the first release on GitHub on November 5. Each entry describes where things ended up, so features that were reworked along the way appear once.

**Old saves reset.** World generation changed many times, so cities saved before this update start fresh.

### Highlights

- Gus can spider-climb and ride moving vehicles, including trains, boats, planes and the roller coaster.
- Trains, planes, boats, theme park rides and zoo animals can all be destroyed.
- Cultists, thieves, preppers and militia now defend their sites, and military bases scramble real tanks and helicopters.
- Tanks and helicopters are proper fighting machines, with machine guns, rockets, shells that reload, lock-on and an aim marker that shows where each shot will really land.
- Goblins chase with real routes: they climb, jump gaps, swim and use stairs and doors.
- Almost every building has been resized and furnished room by room, with stairs and bathrooms where they make sense, and outdoor sites are dressed with props.
- Goblin mode lets you drive anything, and the new Testing Environment spawns any structure, vehicle or enemy on demand.
- The game runs much faster, and can be played on the web.

### Enemies and goblins

**Cultists**

- Robed, hooded cultists now gather live at their altars and shuffle around them until disturbed.
- Come within 15 blocks of the altar or hurt one and the whole cult turns on you.
- They conjure fireballs at range and stab with ritual daggers up close.
- Killed cultists stay dead until the cult has had two quiet minutes.

**Compound guards**

- Thieves' hideouts, prepper bunkers and militia camps now have live guards who mill about until you come close or hurt one.
- Thieves stab with daggers.
- Preppers carry rifles and pipe bombs. Militia carry rifles and grenades.
- Tower guards hold their posts.

**Goblin pathfinding**

- Goblins now find real routes to Gus instead of falling apart in big buildings.
- They hop ledges and railings, jump gaps, climb walls in both directions, swim, and use stairs and doors.
- With a clear path, they run straight at where Gus is now rather than where he recently was.
- They leap at Gus when he's on top of a tall vehicle, and still find him when he's sunk.
- They walk around furniture, and shove it over if it holds them up.
- They no longer perch on stair rails, and they swim lower in the water.

### Combat, vehicles and destruction

**Military bases**

- Parked tanks and helicopters are now real vehicles. Entering a base or hitting one sounds the alert.
- Tanks hunt you and fire shells. Helicopters lift off, circle, and strafe with bursts and rockets.
- They're solid. Moving tanks run goblins down and ram cars.
- Destroyed armour stays destroyed in your save. The base stands down 30 seconds after you leave.

**Driving and vehicle combat**

- In goblin mode, press F (Y on a controller) to get in or out of cars, trucks, trains, boats, tanks, helicopters and planes.
- In a tank, bite fires a shell. In a helicopter, bite (left click or RB) fires a machine gun and stomp (right click or RT) fires rockets. In anything else, bite sounds the horn.
- Tank shells, the helicopter gun and rockets have magazines and reload times, and an ammo panel shows what's left, with a reload bar and countdown.
- A driving display shows the vehicle's health, its weapon and a crosshair.
- Lock on (Q, or R3 on a controller) to tanks and helicopters from your own, and the gun aims ahead of a moving target.
- The camera sits higher and further back in tanks and helicopters. The aim marker follows the real path of the shell or rocket and marks where it will go off: faint when it would never land on anything, and orange at the screen edge when the spot is off-screen.
- Tank shells and helicopter rockets blow bigger craters and damage vehicles, which jolt, smoke, burn and show health bars.
- Crashing aircraft blow craters, and stopped helicopters and wrecks drop when the ground under them goes.
- Driven cars climb one-block steps.
- Motorcycles join the traffic and can be ridden in goblin mode.

**Trains**

- Trains now have an engine at each end and reverse smoothly at the end of the line.
- Each car has its own health. Losing one stops the train and scores 500, and destroying them all scores 1000 more. Subway trains can be wrecked too.
- A moving train throws you forward and up instead of nudging you aside.

**Airplanes**

- Land on any airport plane and ride it through taxiing, takeoff, flight and landing.
- Planes have 300 health and score 1000 points. Shot down, one falls and blows a crater.
- A replacement arrives two minutes later, once no one's within 350 blocks.
- Moving planes strike goblins, cars and you. Parked planes no longer clip the terminals.

**Boats**

- Every boat is a new model: pedal boat, rowboat, speedboat, launch, yacht, trawler, tug, ferry, warship and container ship.
- All of them can be stood on, ridden and wrecked. They burn and list when hurt, sink when wrecked, and are replaced later.
- At sea they follow routes on open water. Others are moored at harbours, quays, dry docks, beaches and rigs.
- Jump while swimming beside a boat to climb aboard.

**Theme parks and zoos**

- Coaster cars, the Ferris wheel and the carousel can all be destroyed. Break the track and the coaster derails.
- Zoo animals can be killed. Wrecks and deaths are saved with your world.

**Traffic and crashes**

- Ordinary traffic now runs goblins down, not just police cars.
- Trains, boats and planes crash when they meet.
- A car hitting a wall faster than 13 blocks a second crushes a hole sized by its speed, takes heavy damage and carries on through. Slower bumps still bounce off.

**News crews**

- News crews now drive out from news stations to cover fires, car crashes and killings.

### Movement

- **Riding vehicles:** Drop on to any moving vehicle and you land on it unhurt, unless you fell from a real height. You're carried round bends and stay on the roof going down ramps.
- **Roller coaster:** Gus can now land on and ride coaster cars for whole laps.
- **Climbing vehicles:** Gus can cling to and climb cars, trucks, train cars, ship hulls, container stacks and planes while they move. Climb over the top to ride on the roof, or walk off the roof with climb held to tip on to the side. Hold climb as a vehicle hits you to grab on.
- **Spider climb:** Gus can now climb on to pillars and one-block-deep ledges. He grabs walls at a slant, and holding climb stops a sprint from smashing what he's about to climb.
- **Stairs and ramps:** Gus can walk up stairs. He stays on ramps going down instead of bouncing, and sprinting uphill no longer breaks the ramp's support.

### Buildings and interiors

**Bigger, more recognisable buildings**

- Cities and large towns are about 30% wider.
- Each kind of site has its place: offices downtown, hospitals in the ring, schools and superstores in the suburbs, heavy industry at the edge.
- Shops, restaurants, banks, libraries and discos get their own roof shapes, with bigger signs.
- Landmarks are easier to pick out, like the museum's copper dome, the whale on the aquarium and the train station clock tower.

**Site sizes, in plots**

- **Largest:** Airports are 14 x 6. Theme parks and nuclear plants are 8 x 8.
- **Large:** Quarries are 6 x 6. Zoos are 5 x 5. Hospitals, universities, prisons, factories, studios and power stations run from 3 x 3 to 6 x 6.
- **Medium:** Bases and dumps are 4 x 4. Lumber camps are 4 x 3. Mines and schools are 3 x 3. Museums, aquariums, furniture stores and cathedrals run from 2 x 3 to 4 x 3. Police stations are 2 x 2 with a rooftop helipad.

**Rebuilt and new**

- **Gas stations:** A branded canopy over three pump islands, with a tall price sign.
- **Fire stations:** Red bay fronts, a taller hose tower and a flag.
- **Schools:** A new larger site with a bus lane, clock tower and playing field.
- **Dumps:** Now sit at the end of a real street, with a gate, shed and big mounds.
- **Courthouses:** New, with a metal detector and a full courtroom.
- **The capitol:** Rebuilt inside with a rotunda, Senate and House chambers, courtroom, statuary hall, library and more.

**Furnished rooms**

- Furniture is now made of small, goblin-sized models instead of whole blocks. Knock it over as you go.
- Restaurants, shops, stores, malls, homes, flats, offices, public buildings, hospitals, venues and factories have laid-out rooms.
- Museums have themed galleries, including Egypt, knights and fossils. Sound stages each shoot a different set.
- Street benches, litter bins and beach and dock furniture are now models too.

**Stairs and bathrooms**

- Every multi-storey building that can fit stairs now has them, drawn as real steps with handrails. They can be smashed.
- Buildings that were too cramped have been enlarged so stairs, bathrooms and doors all fit.
- Bathrooms now appear wherever they'd logically be, sized to the floor, with matching walls and swinging doors.
- The university library has a second staircase at its far end.

### Outdoor sites

- Farmer's markets, camps, campsites, the cult altar, building sites, nurseries, trailer parks, cemeteries, mines, quarries, lumber camps, prepper, militia and thieves' compounds, ruins, dumps, parks, plazas, courts, lighthouses, power stations, zoos, theme parks, stadiums, arenas, car parks, bunkers and wrecked cars are all dressed with props.
- Campsites have motorhomes parked on their pitches, and they can be wrecked like any parked car.
- Campfires, the cult altar's included, are real fires now, but they don't spread and only hurt someone standing right on the wood.

### World, roads and coast

**Roads and traffic**

- Highways no longer slice through big buildings, and they join up properly with interchanges in any built-up place.
- Barriers no longer cut across lanes where roads meet, and highways no longer stop dead in the grass.
- Ramps taper to street width and carry their road markings all the way down.
- Streets have solid centre lines that meet cleanly at corners, plus white stop lines. Trees stay off the streets.
- Traffic flows better, with longer green lights, proper following and lanes that merge before end ramps. The worst queues dropped from 12 to 15 cars down to 7 to 10.
- Fixed a jam where long trucks froze at the foot of ramps.

**Coast**

- Beaches and cliffs now blend gradually, and the sea bed is smooth.
- Places by the water become coastal cities, harbor towns, resort towns or fishing villages. Shipping ports belong to coastal cities.
- The kind of place now shows under its name.

**Parody brands**

- About 40 more parody chains, nine new billboards, and about 90 chains with their own goblin take on a logo.

### Looks

- Distant buildings now match their full-detail look, with their real wall, window and roof colours, proper shading, lit windows at night, and grass, rain and snow that follow the seasons and weather.

### Game modes and debug tools

**Testing Environment**

- A new title screen mode with an empty, flat world. Your own city is put aside and restored when you leave.
- Its spawning menu is its own tab, just right of Game, and only there while you're in it. The Game tab stays the usual title screen.
- Spawn any structure, fully furnished, on the nearest clear ground in front of Gus.
- Spawn goblins, cultists, thieves, preppers, militia, police and civilians.
- Spawn every kind of vehicle, hunting Gus or parked: cars, vans, pickups, trucks, semis, buses, fire engines, ambulances, news vans, motorcycles, police cars, tanks and helicopters. Spawned vehicles keep clear of each other.

**Other tools**

- **Play as a goblin:** A first-person debug mode with a club. Other goblins take you for one of their own.
- **Debug menu:** Now has sections and columns, with switches to hide props, fixtures, caves, sewers, subways and each part of the on-screen display.
- **Structures previewer:** Shows each structure fully dressed, with signs, furniture, rides and animals. A new fly mode lets you explore interiors floor by floor. It now finds a real housing estate.
- **Title screen:** Left and right change tab, on a controller and with the arrow keys.

### Performance

- Toppling buildings and everyday frames are much cheaper. On an RTX 4070 Ti SUPER at default settings, standing still went from 207 to 379 fps and toppling from 133 to 278 fps, and the worst toppling frame fell from 189 ms to 18 ms.
- Street goblins and vehicles are now drawn in batches. With 400 goblins on screen, draw calls fell from 8,688 to about 620.
- Distant chunks that have settled are drawn merged in groups, cutting draw calls from 971 to 311 at a detail distance of 512.
- Places ahead are worked out a little each frame, so the worst frame while moving dropped from 80 ms to 22 ms.
- Crowding checks are far cheaper: about 38 ms down to 4 ms a frame with around 630 goblins. The crowd slider now goes up to 3000%.
- Saves are packed much smaller, and the game says so on screen if storage fills up. Old saves still load.
- Goblin route maps take less time per frame while reaching further, and rescan less often. Smoke plumes only thin out when frames are running long.

### Bug fixes

- Fixed wide double lines on north-south ramps and faint yellow road dashes.
- Fixed the seasonal tint affecting the wrong textures, including road lines, flowers and crops.
- Theme parks now build whole or not at all, which also fixes a world generation crash when two were close together.
- A crash landing on a coaster car no longer blows a hole in the track.
- A vehicle carrying Gus on its roof no longer disappears as out of sight.
