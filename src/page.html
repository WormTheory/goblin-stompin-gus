RENDER DISTANCE

112 blocks

Detail distance: how far out the full, smashable city is built, with its traffic, goblins and props. High settings are slow and memory-hungry.

112 blocks

Far view (LOD) distance: how far you can see at all; past the detail distance the city is drawn as simplified blocks. It never reaches less far than the detail distance. Split screen draws about 70% of both.

CROWD DENSITY

100%

How many hostile goblins and civilians fill the streets (and whether police turn out). 0% empties the sidewalks; 3000% is a mob of about 400 hostiles, which slower computers may struggle with.

TRAFFIC DENSITY

100%

How many cars drive the roads. 0% clears the streets; far above 300% gets heavy.

FIRE & SMOKE DURATION

Normal

How long the wreckage of explosions and collapses keeps burning and smoking.

MAX FIRES & SMOKE

70

The most fires and smoke plumes burning at once; when another starts, the oldest goes out. At the top there's no limit at all. Lower it if big disasters make the game stutter.

DEBUG

In the quick menu: the left stick (or arrow keys / W A S D) moves the highlight, A (or Space / Enter) switches the highlighted item, and left / right changes a slider or weather and season, or moves to the next column on any other row.
Keyboard: [ and ] slow down and speed up. In the free cam: WASD or the left stick fly, the mouse or right stick look, Space / RT rise, C / LT sink, Shift / B goes fast. Gus stands still while you fly.
Flying (cheat): hold Space / A to rise, C / LB to sink, let go to hover.
