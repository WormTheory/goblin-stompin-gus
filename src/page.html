DEBUG

In the quick menu: the left stick (or arrow keys / W A S D) moves the highlight, A (or Space / Enter) switches the highlighted item, and left / right move to the next column. To change a slider, pick it with A first: then left / right move it, and A, B or Esc (or moving up or down) lets it go. Weather and season open a list with A. LB / RB (or Tab) switch to the Show / hide tab, where A picks which switches the first tab lists.
Keyboard: [ and ] slow down and speed up. In the free cam: WASD or the left stick fly, the mouse or right stick look, Space / RT rise, C / LT sink, Shift / B goes fast. Gus stands still while you fly.
Flying (cheat): hold Space / A to rise, C / LB to sink, let go to hover.
