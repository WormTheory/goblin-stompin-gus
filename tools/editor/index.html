Gus Editor

Everything here is saved as JSON in src/assets/. Save writes the file; Build makes game/index.html from it; Open game shows your edits live in a game, before you save.

Keys

G move, R tilt, S size (on a selected box or hit shape) · Ctrl+D duplicate · M mirror across x · Del delete the selected box, shape or key · F frame the view on it
Space play or pause · K key at the playhead · , and . step the playhead · Ctrl+Z undo, Ctrl+Y redo · Ctrl+S save · ? this help

Moving around

Left drag turns, right drag pans, the wheel zooms. Click a box, then drag its arrows. Shift+click picks more than one. Shift while dragging snaps finer.

Animations

0 to 2: wind-up, the blow lands at 1, follow-through. One row of keys per channel; the top row moves every channel's key at that time. Click: playhead. Drag a key to move it. Double-click a row: key that channel (elsewhere: every channel). The diamond by a slider keys or unkeys it.

Posing by dragging

Drag a part of the figure to pose it at the playhead. Body: arm, weapon (wrist; Shift: whole arm), other arm, body (twist and lean; Shift: step). First person: across and up (Shift: tilt and roll; Alt: forward). Hover a channel's name for which way is which.

Hitboxes

The timeline is the weapon's blow, a row per hitbox. A bar is when it hurts: drag it, or its ends. No bar: it hurts on impact. A goblin is hit once a swing.

Models

Each view uses the shared model, placed for that view, or a version of its own (Make unique). Editing the shared model changes every view using it.

Limits

Walking legs and recoil only show in the game (Open game). Panes resize from their edges.
