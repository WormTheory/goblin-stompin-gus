# Players and enemies

The hippos and goblins you play, their moves, controls and weapons, and every kind of enemy. These notes were CLAUDE.md's; search `src/js` for the names in them. Keep them up to date with the code, with targeted edits.

- **Player:**
  - frame loop `frame` (`core/loop.js`). It substeps when dt > 0.05; presses only count on the first substep, and held state like `inp.chargeHeld` carries across.
  - ability switches are each player's own: `ABIL` (player one, localStorage `goblin-stompin-gus-abilities`) and `ABIL2` (player two, `goblin-stompin-gus-abilities2`, first copied from player one's); an online guest's come as `p.abil`. The moves read `ABIL`, so `updatePlayer` swaps in a player's own set for their step; anything reading a switch outside that step asks `abilOf(p)` (and `stompCap(A)`, `poundCap(A)`, `airJumpsMax(A)` take a set). Move tuning lives in `AB`
  - bite / stomp / pound with charge levels (`stompCap`, `poundCap`)
  - dash / sprint / air dash; `longJump` (with `LJ_GRACE`); roll; blast dash / blast air dash; blast long jump (`blastOff`, `crushBall`)
  - rocket boost (`ROCKET_*`); ground spike (`groundSpikes`, `spikeGround`, `SPIKES`); climb; crash landing; fall-damage immunity
- **Buttons on screen:** HUD text names a button as `⟦glyph id⟧` (`btnMark`, `bindGlyph`, `actMark`), and `hudHTML` draws it with the menus' `glyph`. Use these for any new prompt rather than `keyLabel`/`padLabel`.
- **Controls:**
  - `ACTS` / `BINDS` (localStorage `goblin-binds-v1`), `KB_RESERVED`, `PAD_RESERVED`
  - `rebindHTML`, `keyLabel`, `padLabel`
  - controller menu navigation `menuNav` (spatial up/down)
- **Abilities menu (Character screen):** shows the switches of whoever's menu it is (`abilSeat`, `renderAbil`). Rows `#abilList > .ab-row`: a basic move (`.ab-base`) and its upgrade tokens (`.ab-tok`, with a `+1`/`+2` coin) in `.ab-up`; the switches are `role="switch"` buttons, on in full colour and off greyed (no tick boxes), using `data-base`, `data-prev`, `data-excl` (roll ⟷ blastDash). Controller up/down moves between basic moves, left/right along a row (`menuNav`). `tools/ui/abilshot.js` checks each player's own switches and takes screenshots.
- **Goblin routes:** `navUpdate` keeps one map per hippo, built backwards from where it stands a little each frame (`navExpand`; `navCol` lists where a goblin can be in a column of blocks), and `navSteer` reads it: walk, hop, running jump, climb up or down a wall (`gobStartMove`, `gobMoveStep`), with swimming handled where gravity is. `stairPoint`/`roomStep` are only the fallback now. Options' Goblin pathfinding (`DT.path`, `setPath`, read as `pathSimple()`, localStorage `goblin-stompin-gus-pathing`) can switch to Simple, the chase from before the route maps: no `navUpdate`/`navSteer`, no `g.act` jumps or climbs (a goblin on a wall when it's chosen lets go and lands unhurt, `g.softLand`), only walking, wall detours, `stairPoint` and `roomStep`. Suite `pathmode` checks both; `tools/ui/pathopt.js` the menu row. Suite `chase` times goblins running a hippo down in every big building, up cliffs and across water, and counts their blows on a hippo stood on vehicles; `tools/ui/chasecost.js` times the maps in a real browser with a full crowd.
- **Cultists:** an enemy type, robed and hooded goblins from their own pool (`addCultist`, made as needed by `freeCultist`, flagged `g.cultist`, never spawned into town or counted against the street's goblins). A cult altar's venue (`updateCultVenue`) keeps its circle of them alive while Gus is within 70 (`cultSpawn`, bound by `g.homeV`), shuffling round the altar (`cultCalm`). Coming within 15 of the altar, or hurting one, rouses the cult (`cultRouse`); then they hunt like goblins, carrying `['spellbook','dagger']` (`GOB_FOES.cultist`): at range the Fireball Spellbook, through the same `foeRanged`/`foeShoot` as a gun (a spell aims as its conjuring, the orb growing in the free hand, then `fireball`), and the ritual dagger up close (1 damage, every 0.85 s). The fireballs are `FIREB`, flown by `updateFireballs` with the spellbook's own numbers (`WEAPONS.spellbook.ball`); `fbTarget` is what one bends after. Killed ones stay dead until the cult's had two quiet minutes. The Testing Environment's Cultist button spawns them with no altar, hostile at once. Suite `cult`; `tools/ui/cultshot.js` takes real screenshots.
- **A gun at range, a blade or club up close (end of `enemies/foes.js`):** any enemy whose weapons list has a gun and a blade or club carries both: `goblinModel` puts the gun in the same hand, hidden, and its stowed look (`model.stow` in `WEAPONS`: the handgun's holster, a rifle slung on the back) on the body, as `m.wkit` (`{melee,gun,stow,shown,k}`). `foeRanged` shoots only beyond the kind's `gun.near`, so inside it the chase closes and strikes with the blade or club; `wkitState` says what's in hand (0 no gun, 1 the gun put away, 2 drawn, while aimed and just after the shot), `wkitShow` shows it, `wkitTick` runs it each frame (put away when it's dead or calmed), and online guests get it as the goblin's `e[20]`. Police are the one kind so far: the hostile pool's models are built with `GOB_FOES.police`'s list, and an officer (out of a police car, carjacked out of one, the Testing Environment's) is armed by `foeArm(g,GOB_FOES.police)` (which needs the model to carry that gun), setting `g.arms`, which `foeOf` reads before anything else. Suite `copgun`; guards checks a made-up dagger-and-rifle kind does the same.
- **Compound guards:** thieves' hideouts, prepper bunkers and militia camps keep their people as live goblins (`FIG_STYLE`, `FOES`, which also has the bases' soldiers, `FOES.soldier`; pool flagged `g.special` like cultists, made by `freeFoe`, placed by `foeSpawn` at the site's posts, towers hold their post via `g.hold`). They mill about (`guardCalm`) until Gus comes close (rouse distance in `FIG_STYLE`) or hurts one (`siteRouse`). Thieves stab with daggers; preppers and militia shoot rifles (`foeRanged`/`foeShoot`, hitscan with a tracer, hit chance falls with distance) and lob grenades (`foeThrow` into `SHOTS`, `updateShots`, bursting via `foeBoom`). Testing Environment buttons Thief, Prepper, Militia.
- **Military base armour:** each base gets an `army` venue (`armyVenue`) whose parked tanks and helicopters are real vehicles with `TRAIN_BOXES` stand-ins (`isMil`, hit through `milHit`). Entering the base rectangle or hitting one alerts it (`milAlert`): up to `MIL_ROLL` tanks hunt at once (`updateTank`: turret tracks, drives, fires shells) and the helicopters lift off, circle and strafe with bursts and rockets (`updateHeli`). Destroyed ones are saved as wrecked; the base stands down 30 s after Gus leaves. They're solid: `milBlocked` stops a tank or helicopter moving into another base vehicle or a train, plane, boat or ride car (a blocked tank swings aside for a moment, `M.detour`), and `milShove` pushes goblins and cars out of their boxes, a moving tank running goblins down and ramming cars. Suite `site`; `tools/ui/siteshot.js` takes real screenshots.
- **Crews (`vehicles/crews.js`):** a base's tanks and helicopters and a militia camp's vehicles start empty. A real base's `M.crew` is null until a soldier reaches it: `updateArmy` picks a vehicle as before, `milCall` sends a soldier (`crewSend`, out of the `FOES.soldier` pool) from the barracks fronts, and the vehicle waits in state `'man'` (`milManning`) till he's in, or goes back to `'park'` for a few seconds (`M.callT`) if he's killed. Back in its place after standing down, the crew climbs out and walks to the barracks (`milDismount`). The Testing Environment's tanks and helicopters come crewed (`MIL_CREW`). A goblin running for a seat carries `g.mount` (`{o,seat}`, or `{home}` going back), and `updateGoblins` runs `crewRun` in place of its usual round. On arrival, or if it's despawned out of sight, `crewBoard` puts it in the seat (its `g.id` becomes the seat's) and returns it to the pool. The militia's vehicles are `VEH.technical` (a pickup with a machine gun on a post, its gunner turning with it, `vehG.technical.gun`/`.gunner`) and `VEH.quad` (a rider, `rider`, and a rifleman behind, `gun`/`gunner`), never in traffic, from their own car pool (`CREWCARS`, `c.crewPool`). Each has `c.crew` (`{k,drv,gun,go,...}`, identities in the seats; `crewShow` shows who's aboard). The guards' venue keeps them at `CREW_SPOTS` (`crewSite`): put out while you're near, crewed by militiamen from the HQ (one runner a seat) once roused, gone while wrecked till the camp's quiet two minutes are up, removed with the venue (`crewDrop`). `updateCars` runs `crewDrive` for a crewed one (circling the nearest player at its own distance, steering off other vehicles) and `crewGun` fires its gun (`CREW_GUN`). Wrecked, `crewWreck` throws out their bodies (`crewCorpse`). Carjacked, the driver comes out fighting and the gunner jumps off (`crewBail`). The Testing Environment's Technical and Quad buttons use `tenvCrew` (hunting: crewed; parked: empty). Suite `crews`; `tools/ui/crewshot.js` takes real screenshots.
- **Characters (the menu's Character tab, `ui/char-select.js`):** each player picks any of Gus, Gertie, Gnash or Grizzle, kept under localStorage `goblin-stompin-gus-character` and `goblin-stompin-gus-character2` (`CHAR`/`setChar` for player one, `CHAR2`/`setChar2`/`pick2()` for player two; player two unpicked is player one's twin, `CHAR_TWIN`). Picking the other player's character swaps the two. The Game tab only names who you are (`#charLink`). **Gus and Gertie must stay mechanically identical** (Thomas's standing rule): anything that changes what Gus can do must apply to Gertie too. A player's seat is `p.idx` (player one or two: input, split screen); which character they are is `p.gob` plus `p.who` (0 Gus or Gnash, 1 Gertie or Grizzle), and `p.who` is only looks and voice (`dressHippo`, `charName`, `charTint`, a goblin's tunic `GOB_TUNIC`): never branch a move, size or ability on it. Suite `twins` plays both through the same moves on the same inputs, frame for frame, as hippos either way round and as goblins; every line should say `furthest apart 0.000`. `tools/ui/charshot.js` screenshots the cards and checks picking them by mouse and arrow keys.
- **Play as a goblin (Gnash):** goblin mode is each player's own (`p.gob`, set by `setGobP`; `setGob(on,p2)` sets both, player two the other way if `p2` is given; `DT.gob` only says whether anyone is a goblin), so co-op can be a hippo and a goblin. Which goblin is `p.who` (`GOB_NAMES`, `charName`): Gnash or his cousin Grizzle, either seat. Anything about one player's body or moves asks that player (`p.gob`, `bodyOf(p)` for the box, `ptsOf(p)` for the feet, `camPitch(v,p)`), never a game-wide switch. Suite `mixcoop` plays Gus and Gnash side by side; `tools/ui/mixshot.js` takes real screenshots. `setGobP` swaps the player's body sizes, and `updatePlayer` runs the normal player code (`movePlayer`) with `GOB_ABIL` and goblin speeds in place of `ABIL` for that step. `gobCamera` is the first-person view, `gobKit` the goblin others see and your own arm and club, and hostility works just as it does for Gus. Clinging to a wall his feet are 0.06 off its face (a hippo's spot), inside the near plane, so `eyeRoom` keeps the eye `GOB_EYE_R` clear of every block (and his body comes off the wall with it), and `pivArc` swings the eye round the edge going round a corner or between a wall and a ceiling; the climbing lines of suite `gobmode` measure both, and `tools/ui/gobclimb.js` takes real screenshots on a wall. Suite `gobmode` checks it against a hippo; `tools/ui/gobshot.js` takes real screenshots.
- **Guns in both hands (`enemies/grip.js`, numbers in `src/assets/grips/`; the editor loads this file as it is, so it uses nothing but `ASSETS`):** the rig has no elbows, so a gun's pose leaves the free hand in the air. `gunGrip(m,root,k)` ('rifle', 'pistol' or 'tube', a weapon's `npc.aim`; tuning in `GUN_GRIP`, which is `ASSETS.grips`) reads where the posed gun points and re-aims both arms round it (shoulders may give up to `reach`; a rifle turns the body side-on, carried low when not aimed), only for the drawing: `batDraw` (via `gunHeld`), `gobGunGrip` in `gobCamera`, and the Dev tab's recordings call it and `gunGripUndo` puts the pose back, so AI and netcode never see it. A rifle model's root has `gunK`. `tools/ui/gripshot.js` takes real screenshots.
- **The weapon table (`enemies/weapons.js`, built from `src/assets/weapons/`):** `WEAPONS` has one entry per weapon a goblin can carry (club, large club `bigclub`, katana, dagger, handgun, shotgun, rifle, assault rifle `arifle`, sniper, rocket launcher `launcher` (`lock`: held in its sight, `gobSeek`, a vehicle locks on and its rocket homes in, `shotSteer`; `isVehT` says what it can lock), the Fireball Spellbook as a `'spell'`, landmines as a `'mine'` that stacks, and grenades as a `'throw'` that stacks), in the order the weapon button goes (`WEAPON_ORDER`): its name and a line for the loadout (`about`), its `kind`, its boxes (`model.held` in an enemy's or a player goblin's hand, `model.view` in your own first-person view with `fore` for the other arm, `model.drop` lying on the ground), its numbers, **the same whoever carries it** (`dmg`, `every` seconds between uses, `range`, `breaks` (rounds may break the block they hit, by its toughness: `bulletBreak`), `r`/`reach` for a blade or club, `R`/`fuse` for a grenade, a spell's `near`, `cast` (its conjuring, an enemy's aim and Gnash's wind-up) and `ball` (`speed`, `bend`, `seek`, `life`, `r` against players, `rg` against goblins), and the rifle's `butt` for an enemy's blow up close: one edit each, nowhere else), the enemies' side (`npc`: the `strike` up close, the `pose` it aims in, the `aim` grip; how well each kind uses it is in `FOES` or `GOB_FOES`: `gun` `{near,aim,cause}`, `bomb` `{near,far,every,cause}`, `hitBy`, fists as `melee` for the unarmed only, and `FOE_JIT` the most a shot comes after the weapon's `every`; `strikeOf` gives anyone's blow up close), Gnash's side (`player`, which is what `GOB_WPN[k]` is, with the numbers copied in, `cd` from `every`; `GOB_CAUSES` is every `player.cause`), the gun's sound (`crack`, `wpnCrack`, the same from either side; a spell's rising note is its `cue`, `wpnCue`), and whether it's dropped or stacks. `goblinModel`, `gobKit`, `puModel`, `gobWk`, `gobAttack`, `gobShoot`, `gobThrow`, the HUD and the Character tab's loadout all read it. **What each kind of goblin carries** is its `weapons` list, keys in `WEAPONS`, on its entry: `FOES` for the compounds', carrier's and bases' people, `GOB_FOES` (weapons.js) for the street's (`goblin` club, `police` club and handgun, `civilian` nothing, `cultist` spellbook and dagger), plus `pack` for what's carried but never used (a soldier's spare grenade). `foeOf(g)` gives a goblin's entry (`g.arms`, set by `foeArm`, then `FOES[g.foeKind]`, cultist, civilian, goblin); `foeGunK` (`wpnRanged`: a gun or a spell), `foeThrowK`, `foeMeleeK` (blade or club, else a gun's butt, else fists) pick from it. Everything reads the list: `goblinModel` (`o.weapons`, from `foeModelOpt`), the chase's choice at range in `routes.js` (a gun or spell, grenades) and `strikeOf` up close, `foeRanged`/`foeShoot`, `gobWk`, the drops (`gobGear`), `wkitState` (online `e[20]`) and the Dev tab (`devFoArms`). **To add a weapon:** one file in `src/assets/weapons/` (and its name in `_order.json`; the editor's + new does both), and its key in the `weapons` list of each kind that carries it (with `gun` or `bomb` on the kind for how it uses a gun or grenades); smoke checks every foe's weapons are in the table and every entry has a `player` block and a model; guards checks each kind's list (in `WEAPONS`, built into the model, fired, thrown or struck with by the AI, dropped, played in the Dev tab), that no weapon stat is set outside `WEAPONS` and that an enemy's round does the weapon's damage, range and breaking.
- **Goblin loadout (`ui/gob-loadout.js`):** on the Character tab, when the menu's seat is a goblin, `charSecs` shows `#gwpnSec` (built from `WEAPONS` by `gwpnBuild`, drawn by `renderGwpn`) in place of the abilities (`#abilSec`). Per seat like the abilities: `GOB_LOAD`/`GOB_LOAD2` (`{w:{club:true,...},gren:0..GREN_MAX}`, localStorage `goblin-stompin-gus-gob-loadout` and `...2`, mended by `gloadFix`), an online guest's sent in its hello as `p.gload`; `gobLoadOf(p)` reads it, `gobArms` arms a goblin from it (`gobLoadArm`), and a change re-arms that seat's goblin (`gobRearm`). Shared by Gnash and Grizzle.
- **Magazines and reloading:** a gun's `mag` and `reload` (seconds) are in `WEAPONS`; how it looks is `npc.rl` (third person) and `player.rl` (first person, `vr*` timelines: tilt, roll, across, up, forward, how far out the magazine is) in `STRIKES`. Gnash: `p.ammo[k]` per gun (`gobAmmo`; `p.ammoN` is the host's count for a guest, `gobAmmoShown`), `gobReload` (the `reload` action, X / pad D-pad down, or the last round going), `gobReloadTick` from `gobArmsTick` (`p.relT`, `p.relDur`, `p.relW` the gun), `gobReady` gates the attack (empty: a dry click and a reload). A gun with `player.scope` (`{fov,in}`, the sniper) is looked down with stomp held instead of throwing a grenade: `gobScope` (from `gobCamera`) eases `p.scope` 0 to 1 and sets `p.scFov`, which the camera's field of view takes (`ui/minimap.js`), `scopeK(p)` slows mouse and stick look to match (`look`, `readInputs`), `gobArmsHud` draws the `.gscope` ring, and `player.hip` is how far a round strays fired off the scope. Enemies: `g.mag`, `g.relT`/`g.relD` in `foeRanged` (dropped for the blade or club inside `gun.near`), `foeFresh` on arming, bursts `npc.burst` (`g.burstN`). Other gun switches: `auto` (Gnash holds to fire: `biteRepeat` 0), `pellets`/`spread`, `proj` (a rocket through `shot`, `skipP` and `gobs`), `player.climb` (the view kicks up), `player.cycle` (the view box tagged `bolt`, `pump` or `slide` worked after a shot). View boxes can carry a tag (`mag`, `load`, `bolt`, `pump`, `slide`) put in their own group, `p.gb.tags[k][tag]`, moved by `gobViewGun`; `model.foreTag` hangs the other forearm off one. Melee: `npc.wind`/`npc.ft` (a heavy blow's wind-up and follow-through, `strikeWind`/`strikeFt`), `player.swing` (first-person timeline, a sixth value its roll) and `player.body` (third person), `player.slam` (the large club's shockwave), `carDmg`. Reload sounds `wpnClack`.
- **Landmines (`enemies/mines.js`):** `MINES`, laid by `mineLay` (Gnash's `gobPlant`; an enemy's `foeMine` from `foeRanged`, kneeling `FOE_PLANT` s, `g.mineCD`, `g.mineB` the mine in its left hand), armed after the weapon's `arm`, set off in `updateMines` by what's over it (cars, and the big vehicles' hard boxes in `TRAIN_BOXES`/`PREV_BOXES`; any player on foot, its planter only once they've stepped off it, `M.ownOff`; a player's by chase goblins too), the blast `foeBoom` (`mineBoom`, which may name who to blame). A new attack's `pvp*` call beside its goblin damage should come with mines too. Host only: `mineLay` makes nothing on a guest. The debug menu's Infinite ammo (`DT.infAmmo`, the host's online) keeps Gnash's grenades, landmines and magazines from going down (`gobThrow`, `gobPlant`, `gobShoot`). Suite `minehit`. Drawn by `mineShow`, wrapped with `netFx` for guests. Carried by Gnash as a pile `p.mines` (and in hand while he has any); piles are `GOB_STACKS` (field and max in each `player` block), the loadout's switches `GOB_PICK`.
- **Gnash's weapons (`player/gob-weapons.js`):** `GOB_ABIL.dash` gives him the dash (with no direction held it goes the way he's looking). - **Enemy hostility:** a four-step setting, `DT.host` (0 Pacifist, 1 Passive, 2 Peaceful, 3 Normal, up to `HOST_MAX`; saved under `HOST_KEY`, `goblin-stompin-gus-hostility2`, the old three-step key read once and moved up one), set from the main screen's slider (`hostMain`) and the quick menu's `host` row, all kept in step by `syncHost` (which also sets the main screen's description, `HOST_TXT`, to the step chosen). `calmNow()` (below Normal) is what stops goblins, cars, police, soldiers and roused sites going after you: hostile goblins act like the townsfolk instead (`civAI`), who still run. `passiveNow()` (Passive or below) also stops anyone running from you in `civAI`, and a hit no longer puts a news crew, medic or firefighter off their job, though they're hurt and killed as normal. `pacifistNow()` (Pacifist) makes `damageGoblin` do nothing, so no goblin can be hurt (vehicles still break). Online the host sends `hostV` (the four steps) beside `host` (the old three, for an older guest). Suite `marks`.
- **PvP (`player/pvp.js`):** Options' PvP switch (`DT.pvp`, `setPvp`, localStorage `goblin-stompin-gus-pvp`, off at first), read as `pvpOn()`; on a guest online it's the host's (`NET_PVP`, sent in `netClock`) and the switch is disabled. Each player attack that hurts goblins also calls the player version next to it: `pvpArea` beside `hitGoblins`, a loop over `pvpFoes(p)` beside a goblin loop of its own shape, `pvpRay` in `gunRound`, and `pvpHurt` does the hurting (a hippo's goblin damage cut to about a third, `PVP_K`, blows of 3 or less as they are; once per attacker per `PVP_CD` unless given `{cd}`; the killer named in the obituary through `pvpWho`, and `pvpKilled` for the message and points). Shells, grenades, rockets and missiles a player fires carry `by:pvpBy(p)` so `foeBoom` blames them (blasts hurt everyone, PvP or not, as before). With it on, `lockTargets` lists other players (`isPlT`; one in a vehicle is locked as the player, so shots go for the vehicle), and `netRef` sends a lock on a player by seat. A new attack wants its `pvp*` call beside its goblin damage. Suite `pvp`; `tools/ui/pvpopt.js` the Options row.
- **Hit shapes (`player/hit-shapes.js`):** a weapon's `hit.hitboxes` (spheres, capsules and boxes from the attacker's feet, +z ahead) are tested against the goblin's `hurtboxes` (`src/assets/bodies/goblin.json`) by `wpnHitShapes`, which Gnash's melee (`gobAttack`, `gobClub`) asks first; a weapon with none keeps the old `hitGoblins` circle. No weapon has any yet. A hitbox is placed from the feet, or with `on:'hand'` in the hand's own frame (scaled with the rig, as the editor shows it), worked out from the blow's channels by `hitHand` (the weapon's third-person blow, `hitBlowOf`: the same chain as `goblinModel`, `gobStrike` and `gobBodyRoot`, so no model's needed). One with a live span `t` ([from,to] on that blow, u 0 to 2) counts on each frame of the swing that passes through it (`wpnHitSweep` from `gobArmsTick`, trying the stretch passed every 0.02 of u); one without counts once as it lands (u 1). A goblin is hit once a swing (`p.hsHit`). Enemies' blows don't use them. Clips no longer have `events` (the hit flag): a blow's hitboxes and when they hurt are its weapon's. In the editor a weapon's Hitboxes tab has a timeline of that blow (`drawHitTL`, `hitTL()`): a bar per hitbox for when it's live, the goblin posed at the playhead (`hitPoseAt`).
- **Goblin crowds and vehicles (batched drawing):** street goblins (hostiles, civilians, staff) are never in the scene, and cars keep their boxes on layer `BAT_HIDE`, which no camera draws. Their part objects are still moved, hidden and recoloured as normal; each frame `batDraw` copies every shown box's matrix, colour, emissive and snow (`mat.snowU`, set by `snowCar`) into one `InstancedMesh` per box size and material kind (`BAT`, `batKind`). Plain Lambert or Basic boxes batch; one with a texture, transparency or its own shader is left to draw normally on a car, and isn't drawn at all on a goblin. Make new goblin or vehicle boxes with `boxGeo(w,h,d)` so they share shapes and batch together, and never dispose one. Crowding checks use a grid (`GOB_GRID`, `gobCell`), not every goblin against every other. Every pooled goblin (`addHostile`, `addCiv`, `addCultist`, `addFoe`, an online guest's `netGob`) is made by `makeGoblin(o,x)` (`enemies/goblins.js`: o its `goblinModel` options, x that pool's own fields over the shared ones), so a new field every goblin needs goes there once.
