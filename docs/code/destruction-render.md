# Destruction and drawing

Smashing, collapses, big blasts, and how the world is meshed, lit and drawn without stalling. These notes were CLAUDE.md's; search `src/js` for the names in them. Keep them up to date with the code, with targeted edits.

- **Destruction:**
  - `crush(...)` (accepts `{round:R}` for round craters)
  - building collapse lean is worked out from the outer walls alone (`outerWalls`: the first and last structural block along each row and column of the footprint, as built): what's still standing of them on the floor that gave way, against the middle of the whole ring. Stairs, pillars, inside walls and furniture don't count. Snapped to a cardinal direction within 20° (`snapCard`). Suite `falldir` (`SC=none|stairs|east|eastwall`)
  - a place wiped out (`setGone`; `goneAt(px,pz)`, `ptGone(x,z)`) is left desolate: no traffic spawns, parks or drives about there (recycled out of sight), route cars heading for it turn back at the route's end or are recycled unseen and none are sent off towards it, ground highways back out of it, off-ramps into it aren't taken, its stations are dead (`stnDown`), nobody's sent there (`updateServices`), goblins and townsfolk there slip away unseen (`updateGoblins`), and its stadium and zoo aren't brought back. Suite `desolate`
  - water flow (`destruction/water-flow.js`): finite water. A block holds 0 to 8 eighths: `WATER` is 8, `WATER_L[1..7]` the shallow blocks (`core/blocks.js`, `lvl` in `BLOCK`, drawn with their surface at `lvl*0.11` by `meshKernel` and the mesh workers, which draw a water block's side down to the lower top beside it and its sides behind see-through blocks such as glass (set in by 0.004 so they don't fight the block's own face), always its surface even under a block, and shallow water over air or more shallow water as a falling stream, thinner the less there is and drawn on down to whatever it lands on (water falls a block a step, so a leak has water all the way down); the underwater tint (`renderView`) goes by that drawn surface; swum in when the water reaches `y+0.6` (`wetH` in `player/move.js`), waded in below that (`wadeDepth`, `p.wadeK`/`g.wadeK`, walk speed down to 0.45; `civAI` too); a player stood on a floor with water deep enough to float in comes up off it (the airborne branch in `player/move.js`), and a goblin swims (`g.wet`) once the water's 1.05 over its feet (0.8 to stay swimming), held by the surface and never below the water's floor (`routes.js`); `WET[b]` says any water, and the smash, topple, structure and big-blast checks skip all of it). The flow works in 64ths (`WF_FULL`), kept in `wfAmt` where it's between eighths (`wfL` the eighths shown). `setRaw` notes every block turned to air (`wfNoteRemoved`, not while `wfQuiet`, the flow's own writes); each step (`updateWater`, every `WF_STEP`, host only) wakes the water on and beside it into `wfAct`, and moves only what's in `wfAct`, top down: down as far as there's room, then to the sides (half the difference, all it can when water presses on it from above and not on the other, never wetting a dry block with under `WF_FILM` unless over an edge, stopping within `WF_EVEN`). Water that came in from the side waits a step. Natural water in a body of `WF_SEA` blocks or more, or running past the made world, is endless (`wfEndless`, cached in `wfSrc`): it never goes down, only pours into blocks that have been opened (an edit), and presses through the full water it's poured to the lowest few unfull blocks no higher than itself (`wfPress`), so tunnels fill to the top. A door (`BLOCK[b].door`) is passed straight through to the block beyond it (`wfSide`, and `wfWakeRound` wakes through it), and `updateDoors` swings a door open while there's water at either side of its doorway. Blocks beside a chunk not made wait in `wfPark` (retried each second). Changes go through `setRaw` and `addEdit` (saved, sent to guests) only when the shown block changes; chunks redraw every `WF_DRAW` (`wfDirty`), up to 4 times as long between when many chunks are waiting; the depth shown only changes once the amount is more than `WF_BAND` 64ths from it (`wfShow`), and water stops moving sideways within `WF_EVEN`, so a spreading flood doesn't redraw its chunks for every 64th lapping to and fro. Suite `wfperf` times a big leak. Budget `WF_BUDGET` blocks and `WF_MS` a step. In winter (`WX.frozen`) still water with air above, at the heights `freezeWorld` freezes, turns to ice: `ICE` for full water, `ICE_L[n]` (ids 171–177, `ICE_LV`, a see-through sheet drawn as thick as the water was; not solid, but `supportAt` and `groundAt` stand things on its top) for shallow; `freezeArr` thaws them back (when the season turns, `freezeWorld` hands the loaded chunks to `freezeTick`, which freezes or thaws them in `FZ_STEPS` steps over `FZ_TIME` seconds, a hash share of the blocks more each step, near a player only, `FZ_NEAR`; further off a chunk's done whole at once; all through the frame's budgeted rebuilds. `freezeWorld(on,true)` does it at once, for a game loaded in winter), and neither freezes shallow water still falling (`wetFalling`: air or shallow water under it), and breaking one gives its water back (`setRaw`). A fire with water over its base goes out within half a second (`updateFires`, `F.drown`). Suite `waterflow`
- **Big blasts (`destruction/big-blast.js`, design in `docs/huge-explosions.md`):** `bigBlast(x,y,z,R)` (below `BB_SMALL` just a `crushBall`) records a crater `{x,y,z,R,s,n}` in `craters` (`world/places.js`, beside `collapses`, with `BB`; saved, sent with the world) and starts a job (`bbStart`, `BB.jobs`) that `bbTick` (the frame's `blast` step) runs: a ring going out at `BB.speed`, carving the chunks it has reached nearest first, `BB.ms` a frame, straight into their arrays (`bbCarve`, the shape per column from `bbCol`: crater, rubble lip, flattened zone, damage ring; only `hash2` and the blast's seed, so `genChunk`/`flatChunk`/`trChunk`/`seaChunk` carve it again with `bbGen`, before the edits). The edits it carves over are dropped as it goes (`bbPrune`); a chunk it hasn't reached yet is carved first if anything else changes it (`bbTouch` from `setRaw`/`addEdit`), so the order's the same everywhere. Buildings wholly inside the flattened zone are vaporised (`bbPlots`: `collapses` at `gy+2+BB_STUMP`, `rampBldg`, counted destroyed by `bbCount`), the rest are checked once for collapse (`bbSettle`, host only, loaded ones only). `bbHits` hurts players, goblins (corpses past `BB_CORPSES` just vanish), cars (inside: wrecked quietly, `BB.quiet` in `explodeCar0`), `TRAIN_BOXES` vehicles, ships' pockets (`shipSmash`) and mines once each as the ring reaches them; `bbGone` keeps the plan's props, lamps, signs, doors and parked cars out of the flattened zone (through `siteIn`). Effects: `WX.flash` and a white overlay (`bbFlashEl`), then a cloud of cubes in three instanced meshes of its own (`BBF`: glowing, lit and the see-through condensation dome; one entry per blast in `BB.clouds`, drawn by `bbFxTick` from the crater's seed and the time, so a guest's is the same): a fireball rolling up into the cap, the stem, the ground surge and the collar, drifting with `windNow` and thinning away by `BBF_LIFE`, each cube its own shade (`bbVar`) and see-through, gathered by `put` and drawn far to near (`bbCloudSend`, sorted into `BBF_BINS` by distance; cubes on the far side of a water surface from the camera, by `WET` at the cube and the camera, go in `BBF.under`, drawn before the water at renderOrder 0.9, since the water writes no depth (`bbWet`: below the surface, `lvl` eighths up a shallow block; `BBF.under` writes no depth itself, so a cube poking out can't hide the water behind it; the dust settles on water's surface, shallow or not, `_bbSol`/`_bbTop`); the see-through boxes do the same (`seeDraw`: `SEE_U` at 0.9, rings and cones there at 0.95); suite `fxwater`; while a cloud's up `updateFires` hands its smoke puffs over, `BBF_X`, so the two are sorted together); the rim's fires and plumes are lit with `spread` (`addFire`: puffs fanning out, wandering on eddies and slowing as they climb), and every smoke puff, the fires' and vehicles' (`updateParts` kind 2) alike, blows with `windNow` times `SMOKE_WIND` (render/fires.js), the cloud too (`BBF_WIND`), and rain, snow and blowing sand drift its way too, as do the cloud decks (`CLOUD_DECKS`, in `frame`: each at its own `D.wind` speed) (`placePrecip`, render/weather.js: `windNow`'s heading, the view's own `WXK.wind` for strength, added up per view in `precipDrift`; a drop's hidden under cover by `precipHid`: out in the open by one look-up in `shadeMap` (the cover map's columns shadowed back up each kind's slant, worked out once per map refresh or turn of the slant), below that followed back up its slant through the cover map's solid blocks (`C.sol`, 12 below the camera to 36 above, `COVER_N` 64 across so it reaches past the drops' box, made again once the camera's moved 4 any way; a rain streak's lower end is checked too), so the lee of a wall and the downwind side of an overhang stay dry and rain through a gap in a roof falls on at its angle under it; blowing sand only under a roof); the boom, shake and rumble reach each player at `BBF_SPD` (`bbBoom`); after dark `BB.glowK` tints the world's light and the sky orange (`bbGlowSky`, from `updateSky`) and with Block lighting `bbMovLight` puts it in the moving lights (`MOVL_U`); debris off the carve, `BB_FIRES` fires round the rim. Online the host sends `['X',...]` and each guest runs the same job (`netCrater`; `cr` in the world bits catches a missed one). A nuclear plant's reactor (`style:'contain'`) collapsed or no longer standing arms it (`bbReactor`, `BB.armed`: a siren countdown, then R 160, once per plant, `wrecked['nuke:...']`). The rim's rubble is heaped on the column's ground as it is when carved (after earlier craters), never over air or water; below the cut `bbScar` burns grass to dirt, breaks streets and other paving (`bbPaved`: plazas, concourses, courts, a railway's ballast) into rubble patches, burns lawns and pitches too (`bbGrass`), heaps rubble on vaporised buildings' ground floors (`bbVap`) and on what's left of things that aren't buildings (a stadium's stands), all from the plan, `hash2` and the seed; the railway's rails and sleepers aren't laid where it's taken everything (`bbTorn`, asked by `routeMarks`); buildings it brings down get rubble heaped round them as edits (`bbHeap`, host only). Snow: with snow lying the record keeps the snow clock it went off at (`w`, kept by `bbValid`, sent as the 'X' command's 8th field), and `bbCarve` sets `snowBorn` to it for every column it reaches, so the ground and roofs there start bare whether the chunk was loaded, made later or loaded from a save; `bbThaw` takes the snow off players, goblins, cars and `vehEach` vehicles inside the ring. Ships it reaches are carved by `bbShips` (host, a fresh blast only): each column of a ship's pocket, nearest first, cut to `bbCol`'s shape where that column is in the world, through `setRaw`/`addEdit` so `shipDent` counts it (breaking, flooding, its save, guests); one with its middle in the flattened zone, or a third of its deck in zones 1 to 3, sinks. A blast set off in a pocket (`bigBlast`, `shotNuke`) goes off out in the world where the ship is (`bbWorldPt`), and a warhead goes off on reaching water, at sea level. A carrier's deck weapons fall when their deck goes (`mountsFall`). `bldgAt` is null in a ship's pocket. The Testing Environment's Big blast (`#tpBB`, `#tpBBR`) sets one off ahead of you (`bbHere`). Suites `bigblast`, `nukesnow`.
- **Lights out (`destruction/lights-out.js`):** a building that counts as destroyed (`bldgStanding` false) or has collapsed to about its first floor goes dark: `bldgDarkTick` adds it to `BL_DARK` (in `world/places.js`, as the mesher can run first) a few a frame and `bldgGoDark` remeshes its chunks; `buildChunk` (`bldgDarkAt`, per column) stops its blocks glowing, `blkEmitters` leaves them out of block lighting, `refreshBrandSigns` shows its signs unlit (`brandDark`), and `buildBodyMesh` never glows. Worked out from `destroyed` and `collapses`, so it's the same online and after loading. Knocked-down fixtures were already unlit (`drawFix`'s `lit`).
- **What mass destruction costs (`tools/tests/destroyperf.js`):** six scenarios (eight towers at once, a wood felled, a dash through town and through woods, land dug out, a nuke), each frame timed with the frame's parts. `settle` keeps which blocks of each level of a building hold it up (`structCells`, on the plan's building in a WeakMap), as a falling tower breaks into a dozen levels of its neighbours a frame; the crush functions mark chunks to redraw with `chNear` (one to four, not nine lookups a block). `flushRebuilds` gets at most `REBUILD_MS` (5) a frame, at least one chunk, the one nearest a player first. A building coming down during a frame (`collInFrame`, set by `frame`) is a job in `collJobs`: `collapseTick` (the frame's `topples` step) takes its blocks out a column at a time (`collCols`), `COLL_MS` a frame for all of them, oldest first, then finishes one a frame (`collFinish`: its chunks queued, the stump, the falling piece; online, the guest's `['c',...]` goes then). `collapses` is set when it starts, and its chunks aren't redrawn till it's finished (`collHold`), so it stands whole on screen till it goes. Outside a frame (tests, tools), or within a big blast's reach (its rim is heaped on the ground as it finds it, so the order must stay as it was), it all happens at once (`collRun`). `buildBodyMesh` counts the faces first, over a grid of the piece's box, and writes typed arrays. While fixtures or props are falling and nothing's been knocked since the last full refresh (`fixKnockN`), `fixFallRedraw` draws only those again over their own boxes (`drawFix` keeps where they went in `f._r`; props `_iL`/`_iG`) and sends only that stretch. Suites `collsplit` (the same piece and stump both ways, nothing early, one finished a frame) and `fixfall` (every frame as a full refresh would draw it; needs `three` from npm for the matrices). Chunk meshes are made on worker threads (see Chunk rebuilds), and without block light by day (see Block lighting). What's left: `makeTopple` for the biggest towers (up to about 25 ms), `crashTopple`.
- **Toppling (`destruction/topple.js`):** a falling piece is rigid, so its blocks are shared out among cuboid sections (`toppleSections`), and a section whose box is clear of everything (`boxClear`, from `tileTopsOf`: the top block of each 4 x 4 of columns, kept on the chunk's array) is passed over in one go. Only sections touching something look block by block. A wind turbine's rotor (a model) rides the piece that took its nacelle and then drops to lie flat (`windTick`, kept in `wrecked['wind:px,pz']`, `windRestore`; suite `turbfall`). Goblins and cars are tested by turning them into the piece's own frame (`within`, `T.bset`).
- **Chunk rebuilds:** in autumn and winter `meshPrep` copies the nine chunks with fallen leaves taken out (`leafed`, a plain loop: a `map` with a call per block made every rebuild several times as slow). `rebuildSet` only queues; `flushRebuilds` does them at the end of the frame, a few milliseconds' worth, the nearest a player first and then oldest first (passing over a collapse's chunks till it's done, `collHold`). The blocks change at once, the picture can lag a frame or two. Use `buildChunk` directly only when it must be now. A mesh is made in three steps (`render/meshing.js`): `meshPrep` gathers everything that needs the world or the plan (the nine chunks' blocks as the mesh and the light see them, `roadLanes`, the light seeds `blkSeeds`, the biome tint, the ground-colour grid, dark columns, snow, `routeMarks`' strips and the points to light them); `meshKernel` makes the arrays from that and fixed tables only (`blkFlood`, the face loop, `blkKeep`); `meshApply` makes the three.js meshes. `buildChunk` is all three at once. **`render/mesh-worker.js`:** `meshAsk` (from `updateChunks` and `flushRebuilds`) sends the middle step to a worker (one or two, made from a Blob of the functions' own source taken at load, `MW_FNS`, before the profiler wraps them, and the tables as literals, `mwBody`); `meshTick` (start of `flushRebuilds`) applies what came back. One job per chunk at a time (`MW.pend`; asked again meanwhile, `again`; built there and then meanwhile, `meshStale` makes the answer stale), at most `MW_LIM` waiting. Ships' chunks, the debug hidden-features view, no `Worker` (Node) or a worker failing or not answering in 5 s (`mwFail`, then for good) go back to `buildChunk`. A new name used in `meshKernel` or what it calls must be a fixed table added to `mwBody`'s list: `tools/tests/meshworker.js` runs the worker's script in a sandbox and fails on a missing one. `meshKernel` keeps its working buffers between chunks (`MB`, `MBuf`) and must keep producing the same geometry: `tools/tests/meshhash.js` hashes it (with `aBlk`), and `meshworker.js` checks the worker's arrays are byte for byte the main thread's. A chunk costs the main thread about 0.35 ms instead of 1.3 (gathering is most of what's left); on Thomas's PC (`gpuperf.js`, STRESS=8) worst frames went from about 45 ms to 35 and the 90th percentile from 11.6 to 10, while the average was about the same.
- **Block lighting (`render/block-light.js`, design in `docs/block-light.md`):** lights lighting their surroundings at night, baked into each chunk's mesh as it's built. Sources: blocks with `light:[level,colour]` in `BLOCK` (windows, neon, lamps, screens, the hospital cross, crystals), street lamps while they stand (`blkLamps`, gone dark through `blkLampDown` from `drawFix`, or `blkNuked`: inside a nuke's ring, `bbGone`, where they're no longer drawn; the same for `BL_PROPS`) and lamp and screen props (`PROP_LT`, noted by `blkProp` from `drawProp` into `BL_PROPS`). `blkSeeds` (main thread) and `blkFlood` (wherever the mesh is made) flood R, G and B over the chunk and 15 blocks round it (each block a level less, through air and see-through blocks; never following light that can't reach the chunk's faces, `BL_D`); `buildChunk` averages each corner from the same four blocks its shading reads into `aBlk` (normalized bytes; `emitBox`/`emitFence`/`emitRamp` take `BL_r/g/b`, `routeMarks`' points by `blkAt` in `meshKernel`), and `blkKeep` keeps how lit each block is (`e.lc`, two bits) so `blkEdit` (from `setRaw`) can rebuild the chunks next door a changed block could reach. The shader (`nightPatch`, `LIT_FRAG`) lifts the night's `uLight` toward the light, so the day's unchanged; `SNOW_FRAG` too. Options' Advanced lighting (`#blkTog`, `BL_ON`, `setBlockLight`, localStorage `goblin-stompin-gus-blocklight`): off, there's no fill, no `aBlk` and no `BLOCK_LIGHT` define. **By day it's skipped:** the light only shows as far as `uLight` is below 1, so while `blkDay` (set each frame by `blkDayTick`, from `blkFireTick`) `meshPrep` gives no seeds and the chunk is marked `e.dayL` (its `aBlk` all 0, no `lc`). `blkDay` holds while `blkBright` finds the world's light (`todLight`, updateSky's sum for a time of day, times the weather's darkening, the darker of each player's weather now and its target) at least `BL_DAYL` (0.97; 0.975 to start) now and for the next `BL_LEAD` (20) seconds at the day's speed; when it ends, every `dayL` chunk and its eight neighbours (whose light a block changed by day may reach, as `blkEdit` can't tell from a day chunk's missing `lc`) go into `rebuildQ`, nearest first. A change to `updateSky`'s light must go into `todLight` too. `blkDaySkip` false bakes by day as before (the suites that check the light set it). Suite `daylight`. Nothing saved or sent online. Fires and traffic lights light too (the `BL_SIG_N` nearest signal heads' lit lamp, level 9 in its colour, remeshed as the phase changes): `blkFireTick` (from `updateFires`, twice a second, only after dark) keeps `BL_FIRE`, the `BL_FIRE_N` nearest fires and campfires with a level by size and how far they've burnt down in steps, remeshes round any that change (`blkAround`), and `blkSeeds` seeds them. Halos in fog: `blkHaloTick` (also from `updateFires`, 4 times a second) puts an additive disc facing the camera's position (`HALO`, instanced, made on first use; turned toward the camera, not flat to the screen, and faded by straight distance, so turning on the spot can't make it flicker against nearby walls) on the `BL_HALO_N` nearest lit street lamps, `BL_PROPS` lights, `BL_FIRE` fires and signal heads (the lamp `lightPhase` has on); glowing blocks get one fainter halo per 8-block patch of a chunk (`blkPatches`, cached on the chunk's `blkEmitters` list), up to `BL_HALO_PN` of them after the lamps; and everything else drawn to glow gets one from `carLightsView` (per view, from `renderView`): `blkGlowScan` (once a second, while halos show or after dark) collects shown meshes with a bright `MeshBasicMaterial` or a turned-up `emissive` (not instanced, vertex-coloured, polygon-offset, alpha-mapped or depthTest:false ones), and the `BL_HALO_CN` nearest that are lit now (`glowOf`, a texture's colour from `texAvg`) get a halo after `blkHaloTick`'s. Halos only go on lights the camera could see (`blkSeen`, a ray through the loaded voxels where only solid non-glowing blocks block, short of the light's own block; `blkHaloTick` tries every active player's camera, `carLightsView` caches per view and mesh in `p._hv` for 0.25s), so lights indoors don't glow through walls; patches are split by which way their wall runs (a corner's two faces each get one), are looked for out to `RP` (up to 160 blocks) and sorted nearer the bigger they are, so towers get theirs, and the halo shader fades them out at up to 3.5 times the fog's far. `blkGlowScan` runs after dark whether or not halos show, and `blkGlowLight` puts the `BL_GLOWL_N` nearest `MeshBasicMaterial` glowers that have kept still and the same colour two looks running (never one seen to change colour; `userData.noGlowLight` opts one out) into `BL_GLOWL`, which `blkSeeds` seeds like `BL_FIRE` (level by size and brightness, up to 12), remeshing round any change. Halos ease in and out instead of popping: `blkHaloPick` (4 times a second) marks which are wanted in `HALO_ON` (keyed by position), `blkHaloTick` eases each every frame (over `DT.haloFade` seconds, the debug Halo fade slider, default 0.25; 0 pops) and draws the wanted first, then up to 64 fading out; `carLightsView` does the same per view and mesh (`p._hf`, twice as fast so flashing lamps keep their flash; each keeps its last lit colour, so one going dark fades out too). Room in the instance buffer for as many fading out as shown. `BL_HALO_ALL` sizes the instance buffer. Headlights in `LIT_FRAG` only light faces turned toward the lamp (normal from `dFdx`/`dFdy` of `vWp`, WebGL2 only; not `cameraPosition`, which three doesn't set for Lambert materials) and not far below it, so a car on an overpass doesn't light the underside or the road below. `carLightsView` also puts the `CAR_LN` nearest cars' headlights (any not parked, moving or stopped) in `CARL_U`, a cone of light `LIT_FRAG` adds (Block lighting on, after dark), and the `MOV_LN` nearest glowing `MeshBasicMaterial` meshes not in `BL_GLOWL` (a fireball, a blast's flash, a flashing light bar; at least 0.25 across; hidden ones are kept in `BL_MOVC` by `blkGlowScan` so a pooled fireball is found) in `MOVL_U`, a pool of their colour round each. A parked car's head and tail lamps are dark: `carLampsSync` (`vehicles/cars.js`, called from `updateSky`) swaps them for unlit twins of the shared lamp materials while `c.parked`. `renderView` sets `HALO_U.uStr` from the fog's thickness, the debug Halos slider (`DT.halo`, default 0.25; 0 with the Halos on switch, `DT.haloOn`, off) and darkness, and the fog's near and far for their fade. `tools/tests/blbench.js` times it, `tools/ui/blshot.js` takes night screenshots on and off. Lights in weather fog: `FOG_LIT_FRAG` (replacing three's `fog_fragment` in `nightPatch`) fades glowing faces and block-lit ones into the fog over a longer way and tints the fog in front of them with their colour, weighed by darkness (`uLight`) and `FOG_GLOW`, which `renderView` sets from how thick the weather's fog is (0 under water, in clear weather and when the view ends before the fog does) times the debug menu's Fog light slider (`DT.fogLit`, 0 to 3, default 2; 0 with the Fog light on switch, `DT.fogLitOn`, off). `renderView` also sets `skyU.uFog`, which mixes the sky dome toward the fog colour and fades the cloud decks out in thick weather fog, so fogged-out buildings don't show as silhouettes against the sky.
- **Roads in the snow (`roadLanes`, `render/meshing.js`):** snow is drawn by the ground's shader on top faces open to the sky (`aSky` 0 covered, or 1 + the column's `snowBorn` time; `SNOW_FRAG`). A road's top face (`RD_TOP`: asphalt and its painted lines; a manhole's lid) carries a number below zero instead, -(1 + t) at each corner, where t is how far across its lane the corner is, counted in lanes of 4 with the wheel ruts at 1.2 and 2.8 (kerbs and the middle at 0 and 4); `RD_OPEN` (14) is tarmac with no lanes (car parks, runways, ramps' tops from `emitRamp`), which just holds a little less snow. `SNOW_FRAG` thins the snow by that (`vRd`): ruts nearly bare, the strip between them thin and grey, packing down grey above 0.7 cover. Nothing is tracked per car and nothing runs per frame: it's all in the mesh. `roadLanes` works t out per chunk with road in it, for up to three levels (`RD_Y`, `RD_OUT`): a chamfer distance from each road column to the nearest non-road column over the chunk and `RD_M` (12) round it (`RD_D`), then the most that gets to on the way to the road's middle (`RD_H`, half the width), which picks the lanes (under 3 one lane; 3 to 4.3 a lane each way; 4.3 to 5.8 a crossroads, churned across; 5.8 to 6.6 a motorway deck, lanes of 3 from the middle; over 6.6 open). Highways across the plains take t from the plan instead (`nearestOn`'s distance from the route's line, lanes of 3, the hard shoulder snowed over), since block distances are ragged at an angle. A column with a `snowBorn` time is left as ordinary ground. So is road whose blocks have all been destroyed (`rdDead`: each `mapDead` block with when it was noticed on the snow clock, less `RD_DEAD0` 0.35 so it starts part covered, not bare; `rdDeadTick`, snow-tracks.js, looks 7 blocks round each player once a second and queues the roads round a block that's gone; `rdDeadAt` lets a column go only when every block it runs beside is dead, a corner getting -2 - t, which is aSky 1 + t), so it snows over from then like any ground. `snowRoads=false` turns it all off (chunks need rebuilding). The far city's road boxes (`farBox` gives `FARC.road` -5 in `aWin.a`) take about the same average in `FAR_FRAG`. Suite `snowroad`; pictures with `tools/ui/snowshot.js`.
- **Tracks in the snow (`render/snow-tracks.js`):** near each player whose view is drawn, a map (`trkMaps[p.idx]`, made by `trkView` the first time it's drawn in snow) of what's been pressed down: `TRK_N` (512) texels a side, `TRK_Q` (4) to a block, so 128 blocks across, a `LuminanceAlpha` `DataTexture` (luminance how hard it's packed, alpha the height it was pressed at, so a road's tracks don't show on the deck above). It's wrapped: a point's texel is its world position modulo the map, and `trkCentre` empties the squares of 8 blocks (`TRK_T` texels) that now stand for somewhere new, so nothing shifts as the player moves. `trkTick` (from `frame`, after the blast tick, so guests online run it too) presses each visible, grounded car's wheels (`trkCar`: one line for a two-wheeler, four from the axles otherwise, wider on lorries; `trkWheel` keeps each wheel's last place in `o.trk` and draws a stroke from it, `trkLine`), tanks' treads, the Mobile Gear's feet and the players' feet (`trkFeet`, a print every stride, left and right in turn; Gus and Gertie alike, a goblin's smaller), fills them in a sixteenth of the map a frame by `wxSnowFall` where that player is (40 seconds of a blizzard for the deepest), clears it all once the snow's gone, and sends a changed map up at most every 0.08 s. The shader (`TRK_GET` and `TRK_FRAG` in `SNOW_FRAG`, uniforms `TRK_U`/`TRKC_U` set per view by `trkView` in `renderView0`) fades the map out between 46 and 61 blocks from its middle and, within it, makes the roads' ruts fainter (`TRK_RUT`, 0.5), so the real tracks show; further out the ruts are all there is. `SNOW_TRACKS` (picture options' Tracks in snow, `#trkTog`, localStorage `goblin-stompin-gus-notracks`) turns it off. Online each player's tracks come from the vehicles as they see them. Cost: about 0.01 ms of script a frame with the traffic about; no difference measurable in frame time on an RTX 4070 Ti SUPER. Suite `snowtrack`; pictures and timing with `tools/ui/trackshot.js`.
- **Dust, smoke, rubble, blood:** `parts`, `rubble` and `decals` are plain objects, not in the scene; `fxDraw` copies the live ones into one instanced mesh per kind (`PART_M`, `RUBBLE_M`, `DECAL_M`). Decals in a ship's pocket are put again every frame (`decalsShip`), so they follow it; a toppling or falling piece in a pocket hangs off the ship (`shipPar`). `fxSent(M,n)` sends only the n boxes in use to the graphics card: use it for any instanced mesh rebuilt often.
- **Batched models:** besides goblins and cars, `batDraw` batches the signal heads, doors, landmarks and venue models listed by `decoEach`, into batches flagged `deco` that the Structures preview also draws. A box of any size goes into one batch per material kind, as a unit box scaled to size, if its geometry is tagged `.ub=[w,h,d]` (`boxGeo` does this). The scene's own `updateMatrixWorld` skips hidden top-level objects and anything batDraw has just done (`wmFrame`, `wmDone`). `warmShaders` compiles every program on the second frame, so nothing compiles during play: a material of a new kind made lazily should be added to it.
- **Not stalling the frame:** don't call `get` on places that may not be loaded from per-frame code (it makes the chunk, a couple of milliseconds each); check `chunks.has` first, as `updateFlecks`, `holeOn` and `fixCanAsk` do. `plotType1` is remembered per plot (`plotTypeC`, cleared with `plotCache`).
- **Far chunks in groups:** `regionTick` copies up to 4 x 4 chunks a little way off that have settled into one mesh per material (`regionMerge`, `REGIONS`) and hides the chunks themselves, which cuts draw calls a lot at long detail distances. `buildChunk` and unloading call `regionTouch`, which puts the separate chunks back. `tools/ui/regioncmp.js` checks the picture is unchanged, pixel by pixel.
- **Stalls when arriving somewhere new:** `planAhead` asks a place's street questions a little each frame before `setConns` needs them, and `mayLook` allows anything that isn't the chunk loader one new chunk a frame. Plumes thin out if frames run long (`frameMs`, in `updateFires`).
