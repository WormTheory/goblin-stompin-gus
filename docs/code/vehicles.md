# Vehicles

Everything driven, ridden, flown or sailed, and how vehicles fight, crash and wreck. These notes were CLAUDE.md's; search `src/js` for the names in them. Keep them up to date with the code, with targeted edits.

- **Trains:** one per track (`updateTrains`), an engine at each end; `T.s` is the front, but the cars are numbered from the far end of the line, so they keep their places when it reverses. `trainHit` damages one car; losing one halts the train. Riders are carried round bends in the car's own frame. Suite `trainride` tests all that.
- **Airplanes:** two kinds of flying site, both in `world/airfields.js` (ground: `airportColumn`/`airfieldColumn`; buildings: `airportBuildings`/`airfieldBuildings`; layout constants `AP`, `AF`). An airport (16 x 8 plots) isn't on the `BIGP` grid: `airportAt` places at most one per city (`airportOf`, type `'city'` only), just outside it where a street leaves town, the access road on column `B.i` running from that street through the gate to the drop-off; `B.flip` means the city is to its north and the whole site is mirrored in z (`apV`, `apYaw`; hangars get `openZ1`, terminal halls `apFlip`). `bigPlainsAt` asks `airportAt` first, so it wins any overlap; airports of neighbouring cities never overlap (the city earlier in x then z keeps its own). The country airfield (4 x 2) is the last `BIGP` kind. Their planes are in a venue of kind `'airport'` (`vehicles/airfields.js`: `airportInit`, `updateAirport`, `aiFly`): each plane in `V.pl` either follows a timetable (`pl.fly`, waypoints from `airportFlyPath`/`airfieldFlyPath`) or is parked (`pl.park`). An airport's two airliners take turns: a waypoint marked `claim` waits until the other's done (`aiNext`, released at `rel`). The airliners are `makePlane`, the two-seaters `makeLightPlane` (`LIGHT_DIM`, `LIGHT_HP`, flown with `LIGHT_FLY` in `drvPlane`); `planePilot` seats a pilot shown by `planeCrewShow`; `planeMax` is a plane's full health. `planeBoxes` gives each a fuselage and wing box in `TRAIN_BOXES` (sizes from `planeDim`: `PLANE_DIM`, `LIGHT_DIM`, or `JET_DIM` for a fighter jet; the airliner and jet models are built at an old size and scaled up, `PLANE_K`, or down, `JET_K`, to about two-thirds of the real thing, as the game's other vehicles roughly are), so they're stood on and hit through the same code as trains (`trainHit` passes them to `planeHit`). `planeWreck` handles the fall and the replacement. The terminal's checkpoints are guarded by security officers (`FOES.security`, a `'guards'` venue via `sitePeople`).
- **Theme park rides:** a venue (`makeRides`, `updateRides`), whose parts are stand-ins in `TRAIN_BOXES`, so they're hit through `trainHit` (passed on to `rideHit`) and stood on like trains. The coaster stops if a car's destroyed and derails if its track is broken, the wheel falls if its A-frame loses a side (`wheelFall`), its A-frames toppling with it (`wheelLegsFall`), the carousel goes if its floor does. `themeOf` alone decides whether a park goes in, and only where all 8 x 8 blocks are free (moved up to two blocks along to find room); `themeBusy` stops answers worked out meanwhile being cached. `rideSurvey` still leaves out a ride with nothing under it, and only blocks in `edits` count as broken (`rideBroke`).
- **Zoo animals:** `animalBox` gives each a soft box in `TRAIN_BOXES` (hit, but not stood on or bumped into), `animalHit` hurts them through `trainHit`, `ANIMALS` has their health and score. Dead animals and wrecked rides are kept in `wrecked`, saved with the world.
- **Boats:** everything afloat is a box model made by `makeBoat` (kinds in `BOATS`), baked into one mesh, and moved by `updateBoat`: on rounds at sea along a racetrack route that stays on open water (`boatAt`, `boatLane`, `lanePt`), or tied up where `mooredBoats` says (harbours, port and naval quays, a dry dock, off a beach, on the sand, at a rig). `boatBoxes` puts them in `TRAIN_BOXES`, so they're stood on and hit through the same code as trains; `trainHit` passes them to `boatHit`, and `boatWreck` handles the sinking and the replacement. Jump while swimming beside one to climb aboard (`boatBeside`). Suite `boatride` tests all that. Wrecks on the sea bed are still blocks (`wreckBlock`).
- **Climbing vehicles:** vehicles aren't blocks, so spider-climbing one is its own hold, `p.vcl` (only while `p.state` is `'climb'`): a face of the vehicle's box (`vBox`: a car's, or one `TRAIN_BOXES` piece of a train, boat or plane) and a spot on it in the vehicle's own frame, so he goes wherever it goes. `vGrab` takes hold (walking or jumping into one with climb held, or one that would have run him down), `vClimb` moves him on it (round corners, up on to the top, down off the bottom), `vOverEdge` tips him off a roof on to the side, and `vRepin` puts him back on his hold after the vehicles have moved each step. Suite `vclimbt`.
- **Crashes between vehicles:** cars bump each other (`updateCars`), trains and planes smash cars and goblins in their way (`trainBoxes`, `planeStrike`), boats run goblins down (`boatStrike`), any car over 5 a second runs a goblin down, and `bigCrashes` knocks two big vehicles (train, boat, plane) that meet, once each time they meet. Suite `vcrash`. Solid against each other: `MIL_PARTS` are a tank's and a helicopter's solid boxes (a helicopter's tail boom too), used by `milBlocked` and `milBoxes`; `carsOutOfBig` keeps cars out of every other big vehicle, `milOut` pushes a tank or helicopter back out of a train, plane or boat that's come through it, both by `outOf` (the shortest way out, allowing for which way the box faces); a driven plane or boat stops at, or at speed crashes into, any vehicle in the way (`vehIn`, `vehHurt`). Suite `vcollide`.
- **Vehicle collisions by mass (`vehicles/collide.js`, tuning in `CO`):** two cars that meet (the fender-bender loop in `updateCars`, a cheap distance reject first, then `carsCollide`) are pushed apart by mass (`carMass`: `handOf(kind).mass`, the one source of a vehicle's mass, plus a car held on a forklift's forks, so the two are one body) and swap momentum through one 2D rigid-body impulse (`coImpulse`: bodies `{m,I,x,z,vx,vz,w}`, `I=m*(hx²+hz²)/3`, restitution `CO.e`, friction capped at `mu*j`) at the contact point and normal `boxContact` finds (the sibling of `boxesTouch`: least overlap, or the axis it's closing along fastest). The change goes back as `speed` plus `kv` for traffic or `hvel` for a driven car (`carDv`), and spin as `c.yawV`, decaying by HANDLING's grip (`carSpin`, with tyre smoke and a skid over `CO.smokeYaw`). A traffic car spun past `CO.spinAt` stops steering (`c.spinT`, `c.spinH`) and then finds its way again (`carReroute`); a driven one keeps its steering. Damage is from the impulse (`(dv-dmgV)*dmgK`, times HANDLING's `crush` if a kind has one), once per pair per `CO.cd` (`coLately`). `coAfter` adds the flourishes: a tall narrow vehicle hit hard side-on tips over (`tipR`/`tipV`/`tipFall`, lying on its side `carTip` till it's righted after 9 s), one much heavier striking a light one throws it up over the bonnet, and a light vehicle (bicycle, scooters) hit at speed throws its rider off and cartwheels. Walls are the same impulse with an infinite mass (`carWall`, `carWallPt`), and smashing through one goes by momentum (mass times its real velocity into the wall, `carSmashV`, `carSmash`) rather than speed alone; a car sliding sideways (a drift, a knock) feels for a wall on that side before it moves (`carSlideHit`), since its slide just stops dead at one; a driven car's slide and turning never take a corner or side newly into a wall (`carIntoWall`, `carFootPts`), and a tall one's smash adds a ball from the ground so the wall's bottom row goes too (`carSmash`). `setVeh` and `carSpawn` clear `c.wly` (a Gobway's lean, a wheelie), or a car back from the pool as another kind kept it. A hippo is a body of mass `CO.hippo` (a goblin player `CO.gob`) to a car (`hippoCar`: trampling one, or one running into him, Gus and Gertie alike). The big vehicles go through the same impulse (`bigMass` each one's mass, `bigBody` a `TRAIN_BOXES` piece as a body on its course): `bigCrashes` hurts each of two by how much the knock changed its speed (`bigDmg`), so a ship ramming a boat hardly feels it, and a car against a train, plane, boat or ship is knocked and spun by the big one's mass (`carBig`, from `carsOutOfBig`); both keep to their courses. A wreck sliding on (`wsStep`) knocks a live car aside by it (`wsCars`) rather than stopping dead, and a car's wreck keeps the spin it had (`W.w`, from `yawV`, turning `W.h`). Host only; snapshots already carry the result. Suite `collide`.
- **Driving (as Gnash):** the `use` action (F, pad Y; in `ACTS`, read as `p.in.use`) gets into the vehicle nearest you (`drvFind`: a car, or a `TRAIN_BOXES` piece of a train, boat, plane or base vehicle) and out again (`drvEnter`/`drvExit`). `p.drv` is what you're in, and the vehicle's `.drv` its driver; each vehicle's own update reads the driver's controls in place of its round (`drvCar` in `updateCars`, `drvTrain`, `drvBoat`, `drvPlane` in `updateAirport`, `drvTank`, `drvHeli`), and `drvSeat` puts you in your seat after everything has moved. W/S throttle, A/D steer; helicopters fly the way you push relative to the view, Jump climbs and Climb (or Dash) drops; a plane flies in three dimensions (`drvPlane` on the ground and for touching down, `pfAir` in `vehicles/plane-flight.js` in the air: the attitude a quaternion `F.q` and a velocity `F.vel`, the stick setting roll and yaw rates and the wing's angle to the air, the nose following the flight path, `PLANE_KIND` each kind's rates, G limit and stall; lift from airspeed and that angle, `PLANE_KL`, past `PLANE_AOA` or too slow it stalls, `planeStalled` reading `F.aoa`; the old fields `F.v`, `F.gm`, `F.pt`, `F.rl`, `F.vy` are worked out each step (`pfDerive`), and set from outside (the catapult, a carrier's pilots, tests) they make the attitude again, `pfSync`; Mouse-aim's instructor is `pfInstructor`, the mode per player `planeMode` (Options' `#planeSeg`, localStorage `goblin-stompin-gus-planectl` and `...2`, or `p.planeCtl`, which the tests set to 'direct'); the airliner's autopilot `pl.ap` (`planeAP`), the two-seater's smoke `F.smoke`, the jet's afterburner `F.ab`/`F.heat`, barrel roll `F.br` and G-vignette (`planeVignette`, `F.gl`), the undercarriage `F.gearUp` (`planeGear`) and a belly landing `F.belly` (`pfBelly`); it rotates and lifts off past about `PLANE_LIFT`; touching down too fast, on a wingtip, nose first or upside down wrecks it; the sky stops at `H+80`; the gauges are `planeHud`, and an airport is kept, `drvHeld`, while a plane flown off it is still going); bite fires a tank's shell, or holds a helicopter's machine gun (`heliGun`), and stomp (right click, RT) fires a helicopter's rocket, where you look (`drvAim`); otherwise bite sounds the horn. Tank and helicopter weapons have magazines and reload times (`DRV_WPN`, `wpnInit`/`wpnTick`/`wpnFire` on `o.wpn`; the debug menu's Infinite ammo, `DT.infAmmo`, makes `wpnFire` use nothing up, except a weapon flagged `cool`, a cooldown such as the blade's); a round from `gunRound` or Gnash's `gobShoot` that hits a wall may break that block, by how tough it is (`bulletBreak`, `bulletOdds` from `WS_TOUGH`, window panes in `BULLET_GLASS`: glass every time, brick half the time; enemies' rifles and handguns don't trace walls, so they don't), shown by the ammo panel just above your hearts (`drvHud`: the vehicle's health box and `.dammo` sit in `.dveh` inside the player's `.bar`); firing with nothing loaded clicks (`wpnClick`). Getting into a vehicle with someone at the controls (`jackable`: a car's `driver` with its head showing, a tank or helicopter not left `'idle'`) plays a carjacking first (`carjack.js`: `D.jack`, ticked in `drvTick` by `jackTick`, posed in `drvSeat` by `jackPose`, with a stand-in goblin for you, `p.jackA`, and a door, hatch lid or sliding door made for it, `jackDoor`); the driver comes out of the goblin pools (`jackVictim`: a soldier, `FOES.soldier`, out of a tank or helicopter; a hostile goblin in police blue out of a police car; otherwise the driver, as a civilian who runs, `jackFear` in `civAI`, or now and then a hostile one who fights, `jackGrudge` in `updateGoblins`, even in goblin mode). A driven fire engine's bite holds a water cannon instead (`hose.js`: `drvHose` from `drvCar`, the stream in `c.hose`, `hoseHit` every `HOSE_TICK`): it douses `fires` it reaches and pushes goblins (`kv`, a short `stun`) and cars (`kv`, less for big ones) along the stream without hurting anything; stomp is its horn. On a motorcycle your own goblin is drawn sat on it (`rideShown`/`ridePose`), and in anything open (forklift, tractor, small boats, balloon, a ship's helm) sat or stood in the spot `RIDE_SEAT` gives (`seatPose`); in anything closed you're out of sight (`gobView`) but the car's driver head `dh` shows. Speedboats on their rounds carry a helmsman (`g.userData.helm`). `tools/ui/ridershot.js` takes real screenshots. A driven tank pushing into blocks taller than a step knocks out a hole the size of its front (`tankRam`), going straight through at speed. How a driven helicopter flies is `vehicles/heli-handling.js` (tuning in `HELI`): `heliFly` (Simple: an acceleration `M.ac` built up toward the speed asked, tilting it first; Agile, `M.agile`, swapped by `heliMode`: collective, pedals turning the camera with it, the attitude from where the camera points against the hull), the spool-up `M.spin` (`heliSpool`), autorotation below a quarter of its health (`heliAuto`, `M.auto`; `heliTouch` the rough landing) and the main rotor as a weapon (`heliRotor`: goblins minced, walls chipped, the helicopter hurt). The hot-air balloon (`vehicles/balloon.js`) is steered by wind layers (`BAL_LAYERS` by height over the ground, turned from `windNow`; `balWinds`, `balWind`), the burner warming it a moment behind (`B.lag`), sandbags (`B.bags`, `balBags`, dropped by `balControls`) and the rope (`B.rope`, `balRope`: hostile goblins on it in `B.climb`, flagged `g.ropeB`, weighing it down through `balWeight`; `balLetGo`). How a driven train handles is `vehicles/train-handling.js` (`TRAIN_DRV`): one lever `T.rail.n` (-5 brake to 5 power) and the reverser `T.rail.rev` (`trainLever`), speed from power and the brake pipe `bp` (`trainPull`), each bend's safe speed (`trainSafe`) with the slowest ahead on the instrument (`T.rail.lim`, km/h) and squealing or derailing past it (`trainBends`); `drvSub` uses the same lever. How a driven boat handles is `vehicles/boat-handling.js` (`BOAT_H`): the throttle's notches `bo.notch` (`boatNotch`), the oars and pedals (`boatRow`), each kind's right-click special (`boatSpecial`, `BOAT_SPEC`; the tug's push, `boatPush`), planing, waves and the anchor (`boatDrive`; its pose offsets `bo.dy`/`bo.lp`/`bo.lr`, added in `updateBoat`) and the world's wind (`WIND`, `windNow`, read by the sailboat and the balloon); a ship's anchor is `S.anchor` in `shipHelmIn`/`shipDrive`. How a driven tank handles is `vehicles/tank-handling.js` (tuning in `TANK`): `tankDrive` (the tracks' weight, pivoting, `M.cruise` steps), `tankSwitches` (hull lock `M.hullLock`, the shell type `M.shell`, `'he'` or `'ap'`), `tankBloom` (`M.bloom`, spreading the shot in `tankSpread`), `tankFire` (an AP shell bores a tunnel through walls, `apTunnel` from `shots.js`), `tankTilt` (`M.pitch`/`M.roll` from the ground under it), the gunsight `tankSight` (from `drvCamera`) and `tankHud`. The view is a chase camera (`drvCamera`). You can't be hurt while driving; a wrecked vehicle throws you out. A car in water (`carWater` at the start of `carPhysics`, not the hovercraft; `carWaterTop` finds the surface with `peek`): its top speed falls with the depth `c.wDep` and is nothing from `CAR_STUCK` deep, so it bogs down; one off the bottom in water over half a block deep splashes in, floats at the surface for `CAR_FLOAT_T` seconds (`c.floatT`, bobbing) and then sinks nose first to the bed. Subway trains are driven too (`D.k==='subway'`, `drvSub` from the `sub` venue's update; let go, it goes back to its rounds); a boat on the sand or in a dry dock is pushed off into open water as you get in (`drvLaunch`). Left behind: a car stays where it stopped (`c.left`), a tank or helicopter goes `'idle'` (a helicopter settles to the ground), a plane flies on and comes down by itself, a boat stays off its route; a venue holding a driven vehicle isn't dropped (`drvHeld`). Suite `drive`; `tools/ui/driveshot.js` takes real screenshots.

A vehicle hit shows it (`vHitFx`; a base vehicle jolts), and a badly hurt one smokes, then burns (`vDamaged`). Aircraft coming down hard go up in an `airCrash` (a crater, a fireball, fire): `planeCrash`, a shot-down helicopter landing, a driven helicopter flown into something at speed. Stopped helicopters and wrecks drop when the ground under them is blown away. While driving, `drvHud` shows the vehicle's name and health, and for a tank or helicopter a crosshair (dim while reloading, red when locked) plus a marker (`.xt`) at where the shot would really go off if fired now (`shotPath` flies it as `updateShots` would: the tank gun's elevation limits and turret lag (`tankShell`), anything in the way; faint when it never comes down on anything, orange at the screen edge when off it). A tank's or helicopter's chase camera sits higher and further back than other vehicles'. Lock on (Q / R3) takes tanks and helicopters too (`isMilT`, `lockY`); from a tank or helicopter it picks what's nearest the crosshair out to 100 blocks (`drvLock`) and the gun aims at it, led (`drvLead`), while the camera stays free. A car being driven (or hunting) jolts up a one-block step (`c.stepT` holds its back wheels up till they're over the lip, in `carPhysics`). Motorcycles (`VEH.bike`) are part of the traffic mix and lean into their turns. A driven or hunting car that hits a wall above 13 a second smashes a hole in it (`crushBall`, sized by speed and vehicle), takes a big knock and carries on through, slowed; slower, it bounces off. A driven tank rammed into anything more than a block high at speed goes straight through it. The driving display (`drvHud`, `vehicles/fight.js`; run after `placeCamera` and `shipCam`, with the camera's matrices brought up to date, so markers on things in view are where they are this frame) keeps the vehicle's controls (`txt.w`, split on ` · `) in a column down the left (`.dkeys`, one control a line), its own driving and firing controls first (`drvHow`; there's no card about them on getting in, so a new vehicle's controls go there) and its health width as `txt.hpw`; the on-foot hint is hidden whenever `p.drv` is set.
- **Drivers:** every car's `dh` is a whole goblin model sat at the wheel (`c.dhM`), dressed by `dhSync` (from `carLampsSync`) as `dhLookOf` says: a street driver's own look (`makeDriver`'s `look`, from `lookNew` with its own sequence `drvRnd`, so Math.random isn't moved), a crew's, a police officer's (`lookCop`), or a goblin player's; on a ridden vehicle (one with a `rider`) only the head shows. A carjacked or killed driver comes out dressed the same. Online the look goes with the car's config.
