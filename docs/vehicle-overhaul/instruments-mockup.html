Vehicle instruments

Mock-ups for the vehicle overhaul report. Each frame is a game screen: the controls stay in the column on the left as now, the vehicle's health sits above the hearts as now, and the bottom-right corner, where the km/h dial sits today, becomes an instrument chosen for that vehicle. Flight HUDs and the tank's sight sit around the crosshair. Values are examples.

Frames scroll sideways on a narrow screen.
